# Gandi (Gandi SAS / Gandi US Inc.) as the Mosshatch upstream registrar - dossier

Research date: 2026-09-29. All "accessed" dates below are 2026-09-29. No authenticated API call was made (no credentials; I did not create a sandbox account), so every API statement comes from Gandi's published documentation, not from observed behaviour. Raw fetches are in `.../scratchpad/research/gandi/` (scratch only).

## TL;DR

1. Resale is contractually allowed and self-service: convert an Organization to a "Reseller Organization" (free, irreversible, must be a legal person); the end customer is the registrant of record. No deposit, no volume commitment, no approval step is documented.
2. The API (v5, REST/JSON, PAT bearer tokens) covers nearly every RegistrarAdapter method, with LiveDNS included; weak spots are bulk availability (one name per call), no idempotency key, no webhooks (poll only), and "delete/cancel in add-grace" is a restricted feature.
3. Price is the problem: there is NO published wholesale list. A reseller pays the public grid, starting at "A" (retail) and improving only with annual spend (B at $600, E at $12,000). .com registers at $11.00 but renews at $38.38 (A) / $29.39 (E); .ai renews at $179.76 (A) with a 2-year minimum.
4. Sandbox exists (api.sandbox.gandi.net) but supports only .com .net .org .fr and no transfers - only 1 of the 6 launch TLDs can be tested there.
5. Funding is a prepaid balance that cannot be topped up through the API (UI/auto-refill on a saved card); the reseller bears non-payment/chargeback risk (Gandi will not reimburse services already prepaid for customers who did not pay) and there is no self-service cancel in the add-grace period.
6. Terms are reseller-hostile in places: unilateral price/contract changes, suspension without notice for serious breach, assignment to any entity, insurance clause, Gandi emails your registrants directly (ICANN notices), 72-hour unlock delay before an auth code for accounts without TOTP.
7. Ownership: acquired Feb 2023 by Total Webhosting Solutions (now "Your.Online"); prices rose sharply afterwards (secondary sources).
8. Recommendation: FALLBACK, not primary (capable API and permissive resale terms, but no true wholesale, thin sandbox, no webhooks, no idempotency).

## 1. Resale: contract, program, and responsibilities

### 1.1 Which contracts govern (regional variants matter)

- The public "Reseller contract" link redirects to `https://contract.gandi.net/v5/contracts/50047/Reseller_SAS_2020.1.1_en.pdf` (EU/international, version 2020.1.1) from `https://www.gandi.net/en/contracts/reseller` (accessed 2026-09-29). The en-GB page redirects to `https://contract.gandi.net/v5/contracts/42644/Reseller_Intl_2020.1_en.pdf` (contracting party "Gandi International, a Luxembourg limited liability corporation ... Bertrange"; "Gandi International acts as a reseller of the French company Gandi SAS, an accredited registrar"; Art. 17.1 "governed by and construed in accordance with Luxembourg law"), and the en-US page redirects to `https://contract.gandi.net/v5/contracts/62158/Reseller_US_2025.1_en.pdf` (accessed 2026-09-29).
- US variant: "Gandi US Inc. acts as a reseller of the French company Gandi SAS, an accredited registrar." (Reseller_US_2025.1, Art. 1.1). The US General Terms (`.../62153/MSA_US_2025.1_en.pdf`) are between the customer and "Gandi US Inc c/o ORCOM US 600 California ST FL 11 San Francisco CA 94108" and are governed by California law (Art. 27.1), BUT the US Reseller Agreement Art. 17.1 still says "The Agreement is governed by French law" with venue at "Gandi US Inc.'s registered office" (17.3). The Reseller Agreement prevails over the General Terms on contradiction (Art. 1.2). This is an internal inconsistency in the US paper; get counsel to confirm which applies. So there are three contracting entities depending on region (Gandi SAS/France, Gandi International/Luxembourg, Gandi US Inc.), all reselling the same registrar (Gandi SAS). Which variant Mosshatch would sign depends on account region (docs: "You can make sure you see the correct contract for your location and language by logging into your account before visiting the above page": https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/reseller_benefits/reseller_contract.html).
- Registrar of record is Gandi SAS, IANA registrar ID 81, status "Accredited", RDAP `https://rdap.gandi.net/` (https://www.iana.org/assignments/registrar-ids/registrar-ids.xml, accessed 2026-09-29).
- Other governing documents: General Terms (MSA 2020.1.1 intl / 2025.1 US), Domain name registration contract (v2024.1: intl `.../61283/DomainNameConditions_SAS_2024.1_en.pdf`, US `.../61286/DomainNameConditions_US_2024.1_en.pdf`), DPA v2023.0 (`.../53949/DPA_2023.0_en.pdf`), per-TLD special conditions (`https://www.gandi.net/en/contracts/tlds`).

### 1.3 Formal program and requirements

- Program: yes, "Gandi reseller program" (https://www.gandi.net/en/reseller). "A reseller account is free and without commitment." (same page). "There is no cost to set up a reseller account. The reseller can receive discounted pricing on products based on the volume of products purchased" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/reseller_benefits/reseller_vs_organization.html).
- Application/approval: none documented. Steps: create an Organization, then "Select 'Become a reseller.' Check the box indicating that you agree with the general sales conditions for resellers. Click 'Confirm.' ... This action is permanent and cannot be reversed." (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/getting_started_as_a_reseller/index.html). Sign-up entry point: `https://admin.gandi.net/organizations/organizations?locale=en&resellerworkflow=becomeareseller`.
- Eligibility/KYC: "be a legal person and be able to justify it on first request; first, create an Organization and activate the reseller status on it." (Preamble). Individuals cannot convert: "Our Reseller service is restricted to organizations" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/faq/index.html). No further KYC procedure is documented (unverified whether a manual review happens).
- Money: "prepay the ordered Services, according to the specific terms and rates applying to You" (Art. 4.1). Reseller page also lists "Monthly invoicing, a prepaid account, ... payment in your local currency (30 currencies available), and additional, deferred payment options (30, 45 days)" (https://www.gandi.net/en/reseller) - terms for deferred payment are not published. No minimum deposit or volume commitment documented. Prepaid: "Each Gandi prepaid account is specific to an organization" and cannot be moved (https://docs.gandi.net/en/billing/means_of_payment/prepaid_account.html).
- Insurance: "You certify that You are insured against any liability ... (including for example coverage for cybersecurity risks)" (General Terms Art. 20, both intl and US). A startup must be able to certify this.
- Assignment: Gandi "reserves the right to transfer or assign to any other legal entity the rights and obligations" (General Terms Art. 24, intl).
- Termination: reseller may leave anytime (one month notice by letter); Gandi may terminate after a 15-day cure period for breach, or without formality for serious breaches (Reseller Art. 11). On default termination, "Gandi will take over the management of the customers ... and will leave Your customers free to choose another service provider" (Art. 11.3); docs FAQ: "the ownership of the domains would remain with the customers" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/faq/index.html).

### 1.4 Who is the registrant, who accepts the registrar agreement, who sends ICANN notices, who handles abuse/legal

- Registrant of record: the end customer. "Any customer on whose behalf You register a domain name and that You register as the holder of said domain name is declared as the owner as soon as the domain name is registered with the relevant Registry." (Art. 3.1); "register each of Your customers as the owner of the registered domain name(s) on their behalf" (Art. 4.2). API: "When an organization is flagged reseller, then the product is not legally owned by the organization, but by the organization's customers." (https://api.gandi.net/docs/organization/). A non-reseller org must use the payer as owner ("use the same first name and last name for the domain name's owner"), so the reseller flag is required to register for third parties.
- Acceptance of registrar agreement: the reseller. "have Your customers accept the terms of Our Contracts and ... all of the obligations incumbent upon them as the owner ... by inserting them into Your own contracts" (Art. 4.2); "include in Your contracts all the provisions required by the ICANN Accreditation Agreement" (Art. 4.3.2). ICANN basis: RAA 3.12.2 "Any registration agreement used by reseller shall include all registration agreement provisions and notices required by the ICANN Registrar Accreditation Agreement and any ICANN Consensus Policies, and shall identify the sponsoring registrar" (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/accredited-registrars/registrar-accreditation-agreement/2013-registrar-accreditation-agreement-17-09-2013-en, accessed 2026-09-29).
- Reseller website duties (Art. 4.3.2): publish "the rates of renewal, late renewal (if different) and applicable restoration ... as well as the terms of sending expiry notifications", link the ICANN registrant education and rights pages; do not show ICANN/registry logos; forward Trademark Claims notices to clients. RAA 3.12.5/3.12.7 and 3.7.5.6 are the underlying rules (redemption fee must be stated "both at the time of registration and in a clear place on its website").
- ICANN-mandated notices: split. Gandi sends the expiry notices ("1 month before expiration, 1 week before expiration and 1 day after expiration") and the reachability verification to the owner contact email (Art. 6); "The email sent by Gandi must be validated within 15 days, failing which the domain name(s) ... will be suspended". Reseller must relay (Art. 4.3.2 "You undertake to forward these alerts to Your clients") and can resend via `PATCH /v5/domain/domains/{domain}/reachability`. Mosshatch still needs its own renewal reminders (customers experience Gandi-branded mail regardless).
- Change-of-registrant lock: Gandi acts as "Designated Agent" and "does not activate the specific protection against transfers of Your domain name(s) to another registrar during the sixty (60) day period following the modification" (email change, Art. 4); the same is agreed for ownership changes (Art. 5) (Domain contract Art. 4-5; Reseller Annex Art. 8.4-8.5). ICANN Transfer Policy II.C.2: "The Registrar must impose a 60-day inter-registrar transfer lock following a Change of Registrant, provided, however, that the Registrar may allow the Registered Name Holder to opt out ... prior to any Change of Registrant request." (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/accredited-registrars/resources/domain-name-transfers/policy, accessed 2026-09-29). Gandi's clause is the opt-out.
- Abuse and legal process: registrar duty under RAA 3.18 (abuse contact, 24h review of law-enforcement reports). Gandi: "Gandi maintains a legal desk ... legal@gandi.net ... Gandi also maintains an abuse desk" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/faq/index.html). Reseller must "intervene promptly to stop such activities" (Art. 4.3.1), indemnifies Gandi for third-party claims and must "intervene in the context of all requests, claims and/or actions of third parties ... including the administrative and judicial authorities" (Art. 5), and accepts Gandi/registry/court-ordered suspension, transfer or destruction (Art. 12). Reseller/customer disputes go to Gandi's legal department (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/reseller_interface/reseller_disputes.html). Mosshatch needs its own published abuse contact and takedown workflow.

## 2. API coverage per RegistrarAdapter method

Base: `https://api.gandi.net/v5/` (docs https://api.gandi.net/docs/). "Docs only" = not exercised live. All write calls that spend money accept the `Dry-Run: 1` header ("the request's parameters will only be checked; the operation will not actually be performed").

## 3. Sandbox / OTE

- Exists: `https://api.sandbox.gandi.net/v5/` (docs https://api.sandbox.gandi.net/docs/, guide https://api.sandbox.gandi.net/docs/sandbox/). Production reference page: "New! A Sandbox API is now available on https://api.sandbox.gandi.net/docs/."
- Credentials: "you must first create a sandbox account at https://id.sandbox.gandi.net/ and then a Sandbox API key ... This account is not linked in any way to your production Gandi account(s)"; create the key at https://account.sandbox.gandi.net/. PATs: "for use in the sandbox, the PAT has to be created in the Gandi Sandbox Admin" (https://api.gandi.net/docs/authentication/). Both hosts respond (HTTP 200); the API returns 401 JSON without a token. I did not sign up.
- Services on sandbox: Domain, LinkedZone, LiveDNS, Billing, Organization, Mailbox (Email added 2025-07-28 per changelog).
- Limits: "Only a limited set of extension is available: .com .net .org .fr. The Transfer process is not available." Of Mosshatch's six launch TLDs only .com is testable; .ai .io .dev .app .studio (and their ccTLD/HSTS quirks, 2-year minimum, premium handling) cannot be exercised, and transfer-in/out cannot be tested at all.
- Simulation of expiry/redemption timelines, DNS propagation, prepaid funding and price grids in sandbox: not documented (unverified). Route list and rate-limit text in sandbox docs are identical to production (diffed).

## 4. Rate limits and operational constraints

- Rate limit: "you can perform a maximum of 1000 requests per minute from the same IP address" (https://api.gandi.net/docs/reference/). Legacy XML-RPC/v4: "30 requests every 2 seconds. We can facilitate higher levels if needed." (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/faq/index.html). Behaviour on exceeding (HTTP 429, Retry-After) is not documented for the Domain API (unverified). Because the limit is per source IP, Vercel functions with shared/rotating egress IPs need thought (my inference, not from Gandi).
- IP allow-listing: none documented; tokens are scoped instead.
- Auth: `Authorization: Bearer <PAT>` (or deprecated `Apikey <key>`). PATs are bound to one organization, "have an expiry date" (7 days, 30, 60, 90 days or 1 year), can be scoped to product families and specific products, and can be renewed via `POST /v5/organization/access-tokens` ("renewing a token will not make the previous token expire") (https://api.gandi.net/docs/authentication/, https://api.gandi.net/docs/organization/, https://news.gandi.net/en/2023/09/pat-jetons-acces-personnels-api-gandi/). Build token rotation.
- Format: JSON; `Content-Type: application/json` required on POST/PUT/PATCH; `Accept: text/csv` on domain list, `text/plain` for zone file. HTTPS only. Pagination via `page`/`per_page`, `Total-Count` and `Link` headers. 2026-07-27 changelog: header `Filtered-Count` removed and `Total-Count` semantics changed (an in-place breaking change).
- Errors: JSON `{cause, code, message, object}` for 401/403; validation errors on dry run are `{status, errors[{description, location, name}]}`. 202 = accepted-async for register/renew/lock/transfer. Error-code catalogue page (https://help.gandi.net/en/contact/api/api-errors) is behind bot protection (could not read).
- Batch limits: none documented; no bulk register endpoint.
- Safe retry for register (no idempotency key exists; this is a recommended pattern, not a Gandi feature): (1) `Dry-Run: 1` first; (2) persist a local order id before calling; (3) on timeout or 5xx, do NOT re-POST blindly: `GET /v5/domain/domains/{fqdn}` (404 vs found) and `GET .../createstatus`, and list `GET /domain/domains?fqdn=` for the reseller `sharing_id`; (4) only re-POST if neither shows the domain/order; (5) reconcile prepaid balance via `GET /v5/billing/info/{sharing_id}`.
- Funding constraint: the API has no top-up endpoint. Prepaid is credited in the UI or by auto-refill from the default saved credit card ("Prepaid account automatic crediting"), with an email low-balance alert (https://docs.gandi.net/en/billing/means_of_payment/prepaid_account.html). General Terms: an order that cannot be paid "will remain pending for a maximum period of two months, until the Prepaid Account is sufficiently funded" (Annex 1 Art. 4). Mosshatch's "human passkey approves anything that costs money" flow sits on top of a pre-funded float, not a per-purchase charge to Gandi.
- Statuses/incidents (https://status.gandi.net/api/v2/incidents.json, 50 most recent, 2025-09-06..2026-09-10): "PUBLIC API v5" listed on 2026-09-07 "Slowness on change_owner domain operations" (minor) and 2025-11-27 "Domain update delayed"; registry-connectivity incidents for individual ccTLDs (.cz 2026-07-10, .pt 2026-06-22, .fi 2026-04-17, .fr-family 2026-04-16, .tc 2026-01); "Domain name availability issue" 2026-07-21; LiveDNS "update latency" 2026-05-12. No incident touching .com/.ai/.io/.dev/.app/.studio registration in that window. Current state: "All Systems Operational".

## 5. Wholesale prices (USD, per year unless noted)

Basis: Gandi PUBLIC price grid, US region, USD, excluding VAT (`https://www.gandi.net/en-US/domain/tld/<tld>?c=US&currency=USD&taxes=no&grid=A..E`, accessed 2026-09-29; list view cross-check `https://www.gandi.net/en-US/domain/tld?prefix=<letter>` gave identical A-grid numbers). There is no separate wholesale price list; a reseller pays the same grid as everyone, determined by spend: "New accounts or those who have made no purchases in the same year have A rates by default. ... ## 6. Lifecycle

- Deadlines per TLD (https://docs.gandi.net/en/domain_names/renew/deadlines.html, accessed 2026-09-29): .com/.dev/.app/.studio: "Late renewal possible until 45 days after expiration", "Deleted by Gandi 45 days after expiration", "Restoration possible between 45 days after and 75 days after expiration", deleted by registry 80 days after (.io: 90; .ai: late renewal 30 days, restoration 30-89 days, deleted by registry 89 days). Pending-delete after restoration: 5 days (.com/.dev/.app/.studio), 15 days (.ai/.io) (price pages).
- Auto-renew: Gandi does not auto-renew by default: "Domain names are not automatically renewed at Gandi unless you have specifically activated automatic renewal ... you will not be charged, your domain registration will expire". Enable per domain via `PATCH /autorenew`; attempts at 31, 15 and 1 days before the last renewal date (60 days for app/dev/org/net/info); "Once an automatic renewal is launched, the process cannot be canceled and a refund will not be offered." Funded from prepaid (https://docs.gandi.net/en/domain_names/renew/automatic_renewal.html).
- Redemption: restore is a paid operation (`POST /domains/{domain}/restore`), price in section 5; "Restoration rates are higher than renewal rates, because Gandi is charged more by the Registries" (https://helpdesk.gandi.net/hc/en-us/articles/14001605126044-Restoring-a-domain).
- Add-grace/refunds: docs FAQ - ".com registrations can be canceled up to day 5"; "Some transactions ... are non-refundable once completed" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/faq/index.html). Only .com's 5-day figure is stated by Gandi; AGP for the other five TLDs is not documented (unverified). Reseller Art. 8: "We undertake to reimburse You for any operation that could not be completed, provided that this operation can still be cancelled and reimbursed by the Registry." But no self-service cancel: API `DELETE` is "a restricted feature" and the docs say early deletion needs "the domain name deletion form (and all the required proof of ID ...) ... by fax, email, or postal mail" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/domain_names/faq/deletion.html). Non-payment risk: "the risk of non-payment must be borne by the reseller"; "We will not be able to reimburse You for the services ... You will have prepaid on behalf of Your customers, even if the latter have not paid You" (Reseller Art. 8). Stripe chargebacks after registration are therefore Mosshatch's loss.
- 60-day transfer lock: "Domains generally cannot be transferred within 60 days of registration or transfer" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/domain_names/faq/transfers.html); ICANN Transfer Policy 3.7.5/3.7.6 (60 days from creation, 60 days after a transfer). After change of registrant Gandi does NOT add the 60-day lock (Designated Agent opt-out, see 1.4). ccTLDs differ: .io needs >= 60 days since creation; the .ai page requires that the domain "does not expire soon"; .ai has no anonymized-email option. Transfer-lock availability for .ai/.io is not documented per TLD (Gandi says "most two letter extensions ... do not" have a lock; check `can_tld_lock` / `GET /v5/domain/tlds/ai` once authenticated - unverified).
- Auth code delivery: in the account (owner/admin only), API `authinfo` field. Web UI unlock delay: "For security reasons, the unlocking process for your domain may take up to 72 hours ... this delay does not apply if your user account is secured with multi-factor authentication using TOTP" (https://helpdesk.gandi.net/hc/en-us/articles/18536928521244-Why-do-I-have-to-wait-72-hours-before-receiving-my-domain-s-authorization-code). A customer blog (Aug 2025) confirms 72h and a rejected code for a .be domain (https://blog.yuo.be/2025/08/28/the-pain-of-transferring-a-domain-from-gandi-net/). Reseller customers have no Gandi login; the reseller performs unlock and hands over the code, or "release[s]" the customer/domain so the owner can log in (https://docs.gandi.net/en/reseller/reseller_interface/release_domains.html; org action `release_resellee` in the Organization API).
- Transfer-in: confirmation emails go to the owner; Gandi's .com page says the process "can take from 1 to 10 days on average"; gaining flow needs the authinfo, 60-day age, unlocked; domain then renews 1 year (2 years per the .ai page). Outbound: 5-day auto-approval, no fee documented, mail service stops on transfer (irrelevant to Mosshatch).
- Registrant change: `POST /changeowner/{domain}` is "not always a free operation" for some TLDs; .com/.ai/.io/.dev/.app/.studio owner change is listed as FREE and "does not change the expiration date" (price pages). Owner-email change triggers a fresh reachability verification (15 days) - a suspension risk.
- ICANN Transfer Policy text was updated 21 Feb 2024 for the Registration Data Policy and "must implement no later than 21 August 2025" (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/accredited-registrars/resources/domain-name-transfers); another registrar announced a 4 August 2026 platform change moving transfer approval from the Admin Contact to the registrant (https://realtimeregister.com/blogs/icann-updates-gtld-transfer-policy-from-4-august-2026). Whether Gandi changed its transfer emails accordingly was not verified.

## 7. WHOIS privacy and RDAP redaction

- Free and on by default: "Gandi's privacy service is a free and automatic service ... Hidden WHOIS Data: This option, activated by default ... All other information will display 'redacted for privacy'" and "Anonymized Email Address: This option, activated by default, replaces your email address ... with ... xxxxxxxxx@contact.gandi.net" (https://docs.gandi.net/en/domain_names/common_operations/whois_privacy.html).
- API control: `data_obfuscated` (default true, "Learn more about WHOIS privacy") and `mail_obfuscated` on owner/admin/tech/bill in create, change-owner, contacts (https://api.gandi.net/docs/domains/). The `icann_contract_accept` flag ("authorize the registrar, Gandi, to be the 'Designated Agent'") is required when changing the owner email.
- Exceptions: the anonymized-email option is not supported for ".ai, .ar, .au, .be, .bo, .br ..." (list on the docs page); for .ai the owner's real email is therefore presumably what reaches the registry (my inference, unverified). Several other ccTLDs display registry-provided WHOIS. .com/.io/.dev/.app/.studio are not on either exception list.
- Observed RDAP (live query of `https://rdap.gandi.net/domain/gandi.net`, accessed 2026-09-29): registrant/admin/tech/billing entities have `fn: "Redacted for Privacy"`, an `@contact.gandi.net` relay email, and remarks "REDACTED FOR PRIVACY / object redacted due to authorization". This was a Gandi-owned domain, so it is indicative only for customer domains.

## 8. Notifications

- Webhooks/callbacks: none. Zero occurrences of "webhook" or "callback" in the v5 API docs, the docs.gandi.net search index and other API sections (Template, LinkedZone) checked 2026-09-29.
- Async work returns 202 and must be polled: `createstatus` (register), `GET /transferin/{domain}` (transfer-in), `GET /changeowner/{domain}` (owner change), `GET /domains/{domain}` (lock, renew, DNS changes visible in status/dates). Expiry: poll `GET /domain/domains` (returns `dates.registry_ends_at`, `status`, `autorenew`) and drive your own reminders. DNS: LiveDNS record writes return immediately with 200/201 and a message (no task id documented), unlike domain operations; "Live DNS update latency" appeared as a status incident 2026-05-12.
- Email notifications go to humans only (billing contact renewal confirmations, prepaid low-balance alerts, registrant FOA/verification emails). Mosshatch must parse or ignore them; there is no machine channel.

## 9. Red flags

1. Pricing: public retail grid is the reseller price; big renewal premiums (.com $38.38 renew vs $11.00 register at A). Post-acquisition increases documented by secondary sources: e.g. ".com domain prices will jump by 34%, .net by 45%, .org by 32%" (https://chatting.neocities.org/posts/2023-gandi-pricing); a customer reports a .be renewal quote of EUR 38.38 vs EUR 14.96 in 2022 (https://blog.yuo.be/2025/08/28/the-pain-of-transferring-a-domain-from-gandi-net/). Primary: 2021 pass-through of a Verisign increase within weeks (https://news.gandi.net/en/2021/07/price-increase-on-com-domain-name-prices-starting-september-1-2021/).
2. Unilateral change: General Terms Art. 23 "Our Contracts, including the rates of Our Services, are subject to modifications"; new versions can "come into force during the course of a Service Agreement and will be notified to You when You log in"; disagreement remedy is to terminate. Art. 24 assignment to any entity. Reseller Art. 15: Gandi may "change, modify or stop providing, at any time ... all or part of the optional ancillary Services."
3. Suspension/termination: Reseller Art. 11.2.1: breaches uncured "within fifteen (15) calendar days" lead to suspension/termination of the Reseller Organization "without further formality and without You being entitled to any compensation"; serious breaches, including "the fact of generating, through negligence, lack of management or poor management of many complaints from Your customers", can lead to suspension "without notice". General Terms 3.6/12.4 allow suspension/deletion without notice for legal orders. Art. 12: Gandi may suspend, modify, transfer or destroy domains on payment reversal or disputes.
4. Payment risk on reseller: non-refundable prepaid orders (see 6); insurance certification; broad indemnity (Art. 5).
5. Not white-label: Gandi emails registrants; contract requires disclosing Gandi as registrar; Gandi may also contact your clients if you fail to answer within 48 hours (Art. 6) or object to their rights, and "propose to the client to dissociate the domain name(s) from Your Reseller Organization".
6. Ownership/direction: acquired Feb 2023 by Total Webhosting Solutions BV, forming Your.Online; "Gandi.net will remain autonomous" per the deal announcement (https://domainnamewire.com/2023/03/02/total-web-solutions-acquires-domain-registrar-gandi-forming-new-entity/; Gandi About page: "In 2023, Gandi.net and Total Webhosting Solutions (TWS) join forces and announce the creation of Your.Online" https://www.gandi.net/en-US/about-us). Contract paper is dated: intl reseller contract v2020.1.1 and MSA v2020.1.1 are still the "current version".
7. API hygiene: in-place breaking change (2026-07-27, `Filtered-Count` removed); API keys deprecated in favour of expiring PATs (rotation burden); owner contact updates via PATCH removed 2026-01-28; deletion "restricted"; no idempotency keys; no webhooks; sandbox is thin.
8. Operational: several ccTLD-specific outages in 2026 (status API), storage incidents on hosting (not relevant), 72-hour unlock delay (Aug 2025 blog), Cloudflare bot protection on helpdesk pages (403 to non-browser clients; docs and API domains were reachable).
9. Security history: July 2017 - 751 ccTLD domains redirected after credentials to a third-party technical provider were intercepted (https://domainnamewire.com/2017/07/15/bad-guys-get-gandi-nets-password-technical-provider-redirect-domains/). Old, but relevant to the passkey/KMS story. ICANN compliance history for Gandi SAS was not reviewed.
10. Developer complaints: limited evidence gathered (web-search budget was exhausted); GitHub issue trackers of client libraries were not reviewed.

## 10. Recommendation: FALLBACK (not primary, not reject)

Gandi is a capable, self-service upstream whose paper explicitly authorises what Mosshatch wants (independent company, own brand and price, API ordering, end customer as registrant, no deposit or volume commitment, free WHOIS privacy and included DNS with per-RRset CRUD). It fails the "wholesale plus one flat fee" premise: it publishes no wholesale list, a new reseller pays retail-grid "A" rates (for example .com renew $38.38, .ai $179.76 in 2-year units, .io $74.99) and only reaches "E" rates (.com renew $29.39) after $12,000 of annual purchases, so Mosshatch's flat fee would sit on top of a high, unilaterally changeable cost basis and renewals would dominate margin. Engineering friction is also real: single-name availability checks, no idempotency key or webhooks (poll everything), prepaid float that cannot be funded by API, a sandbox limited to .com/.net/.org/.fr without transfers (five of six launch TLDs untestable), a 72-hour unlock delay in the UI, and a restricted delete/AGP-cancel path while Mosshatch carries chargeback risk. Because none of these is a legal blocker and the API otherwise maps well onto every RegistrarAdapter method, Gandi is a reasonable second registrar behind whichever provider offers true wholesale pricing, richer test environments and event delivery; it should not be the launch primary unless a negotiated rate (support will "temporarily lower your rate" for a big sales push, per the reseller benefits page) closes the price gap. Keep the adapter provider-neutral, and budget a legal read of the US-vs-EU contract mismatch, the insurance clause and the Art. 4.3.2 website obligations before signing.
