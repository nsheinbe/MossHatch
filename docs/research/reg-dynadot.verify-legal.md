# Verification notes: reg-dynadot, lens = LEGAL / PROGRAM

Verifier run date: 2026-09-29. Every source below was fetched fresh by me (curl with browser UA through the pre-configured proxy, or headless Chromium for the JS-rendered tables). I did not reuse the analyst's captures. My raw captures are in `working-directory/research/vl-dynadot/`. WebSearch budget was already exhausted (200/200), so no search-engine checks were possible; I used sitemaps and direct fetches instead. No account was created; nothing behind login or an application form was read.

## Bottom line

The analyst's legal reading is accurate on the quotes and on the overall shape ("conditional yes; FALLBACK; get written clarification"). I could re-derive every quoted clause from the primary text. Corrections and omissions that matter:

1. The RAA citations are to the superseded 2013-09-17 text. The agreement in force is the 21 Jan 2024 version (global amendment effective 5 Apr 2024). 3.12 and 3.7.7 are unchanged, but 3.18 was rewritten (new DNS Abuse mitigation duty).
2. The analyst says program requirements (deposit, quota, approval time) are unpublished. Dynadot's own blog of 2025-12-26 publishes them: no registration fees, no mandatory deposits, no minimum deposits, no sales quotas, no pre-purchase requirement, approval "usually instant". It is marketing, not contract, but it should be cited. The same post says API integration into "your own website, app, or platform" is a supported use, which materially softens (but does not cure) the ToU s13.1 tension.
3. Clauses the analyst did not list: Dynadot ToU s3.1(d) (Mosshatch's own customer terms must say Dynadot's ToU prevail); a broad indemnity in the Reseller Agreement (and GDG ToU s3.2, s14.5 registry indemnity within 30 days); one-way attorney-fee shifting; the exclusion of all provider liability (ToU s3.4); Dynadot as third-party beneficiary; no DPA / no GDPR / no international-transfer terms anywhere; precedence clause s3.13 (marketing cannot override the ToU); conflicting "entire agreement" clauses; GDG ToU s2.2 (15-day response window on accuracy inquiries); Dynadot ToU s11.3 designated-agent clause covers only "You", not "Your customer" (the GDG version adds "and Your customer's").
4. The "expired reseller domains may be auctioned" item is not merely unverified: GDG ToU s8.4/8.5 (the reseller-oriented paper) permit it with no reseller carve-out, and Dynadot's help says a domain "not renewed by the registrant and past its grace renewal period ... will go to expired auction".

## Claim-by-claim

### C1. Resale under own brand/price/checkout, ordering via API, is expressly contemplated by a Reseller Program (verdict: CONFIRMED)
- https://www.dynadot.com/domain/reseller-program (fetched 2026-09-29, HTTP 200). Verbatim: "The Dynadot Reseller Program allows you to sell domain name registrations and domain management under your own brand using Dynadot's infrastructure"; "Complete Pricing Control: As the domain reseller, you'll have flexibility on domain registration, transfer, and renewal prices, along with deciding what prices are shown to your customers."; "Separate Registrar Accreditation: Domain names registered under your reseller account won't be registered under Dynadot, so your customers won't be aware of the registrar connection."; opening line "Implement domain registrations into your business or online platform using our domain reseller program."
- https://www.globaldomaingroup.com/ : "A fully white-label solution, ensuring domains registered through GDG are not associated with a popular registrar."; "Let your customers acquire domain names directly from your brand"; "Expansive API".
- Additional primary evidence the analyst did not cite: https://www.dynadot.com/blog/dynadot-domain-reseller-affiliate (Dec 26, 2025): "integrate your own website, app, or platform using our powerful, high-speed API"; "You sit between Dynadot and your customers, offering domains under your own brand thanks to our white-label platform, Global Domain Group (GDG)". Storefront help (https://www.dynadot.com/help/question/create-a-storefront, updated 2025/11/26): reseller sets its own Stripe account, "The payment will not go through Dynadot" - i.e. reseller-owned checkout is the designed model.
- Caveat: none of this is contractual. Dynadot ToU s3.13 and GDG ToU s3.13: "To the extent that anything in or associated with this website or App is in conflict or inconsistent with this Agreement, this Agreement shall take precedence." So marketing cannot override ToU s13.1.

### C2. Governing contract is the Dynadot Reseller Agreement v2023-08-15 between Global Domain Group LLC and the reseller; quoted flow-downs, 7-day liability, termination clause (CONFIRMED)
- https://www.dynadot.com/reseller-agreement, "Version date: 2023-08-15". All five quoted sentences match verbatim: registration agreement must "shall identify GDG as the registrar or provide a means for identifying GDG as the sponsoring registrar"; "You shall identify GDG as the sponsoring registrar upon inquiry from Your customer."; "You shall remain liable for harm caused by the wrongful use of a domain name, unless You disclose the current contact information provided by Your customer and the identity of Your customer within seven (7) days ..."; "GDG may, in its absolute and sole discretion, terminate this Agreement without notice, transfer any and all of Your customer accounts to Dynadot accounts and grant each customer independent control over the respective account and the respective domain name."; amendment "from time to time without specific advance notice to You"; acceptance of Dynadot ToU "amended by Dynadot from time to time without specific advance notice".
- Also present and confirmed: no ICANN logo / "otherwise represent Yourself as accredited by ICANN"; registrant-education link duty; Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities Specification duty; privacy-consent duty; California San Mateo venue; jury waiver; GDG-only fee shifting; six-month limitation.
- The analyst's "not in the agreement: pricing, wholesale discounts, minimum deposit, volume commitments, SLA, price-change notice, data-processing terms" is CONFIRMED (I read the full 57-line text: no term, no fees, no precedence clause, no DPA).
- MISSED by analyst: (a) indemnity - "You agree to indemnify, defend and hold harmless GDG and its partners, attorneys, directors, employees, agents, staff and affiliates from any liability, loss, claim and expense, including reasonable attorney fees, related to Your violation of this Agreement, use of Dynadot's website and participation in GDG's distribution channel for domain name registrations." (b) "Dynadot shall be deemed a third-party beneficiary ... entitled to enforce ... as if it was party hereto." (c) "If GDG prevails in any litigation against You then GDG will receive from You all costs and reasonable, actual attorney fees" - one-way. (d) You "represent that ... neither Your registration of a domain name nor the manner in which a domain name under Your control is ... used shall result in the infringement of the legal rights of any third party." (e) Proxy/privacy accreditation clause: GDG may prohibit you from knowingly accepting registrations from unaccredited privacy/proxy providers; until then you must comply with the Specification on Privacy and Proxy Registrations - so Mosshatch must not run its own privacy/proxy layer over customer registrations without checking that spec.
- Note the stale link: the agreement (and GDG ToU s3.6) point to http://www.icann.org/en/registrars/registrant-rights-responsibilities-en.htm, which 301-redirects to an ICANN page titled "registrant rights and responsibilities under the 2009 registrar accreditation agreement". Dynadot's own footer uses https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/benefits-2013-09-16-en (HTTP 200). Mosshatch should link ICANN's current page and confirm with GDG which URL satisfies them.

### C3. GDG Terms of Use v2026-08-18: Part I s3 RESALE, s4.2, s7.3, s11.3, s1.4, s6.1, s12.1 (CONFIRMED)
- https://www.globaldomaingroup.com/terms-of-service, "Version date: 2026-08-18". s3.1-3.10 repeat the ICANN flow-downs (s3.8 privacy consents "whose personal data You supply to GDG"; s3.9 seven-day disclosure). s4.2 "assume all liabilities arising from the use of Your account, including ... caused, directly or indirectly, by unauthorized parties." s7.3(a)-(e) sole-and-absolute-discretion suspension/termination including (d) "while investigating". s11.3 "You expressly appoint GDG as Your and Your customer's Designated Agent to approve any Change of Registrant request and to opt out of a 60-day lock." s1.4 amendment without notice. s6.1 renewal-fee disclaimer. s12.1(b),(c) API text identical to Dynadot ToU s13.1.
- MISSED by analyst: s1.2 "entire and only agreement" (conflicts with the 2023 Reseller Agreement's own entire-agreement clause; neither states which prevails); s1.5 third-party beneficiaries (GDG affiliates, each registry, Verisign); s2.2 (failure to respond >15 days to GDG accuracy inquiries, or willful inaccuracy, is a material breach and basis for suspension/cancellation; Mosshatch must relay inquiries and correct data within 7 days); s3.2 indemnity; s3.4 (no liability whatsoever, no consequential damages, disclaims loss from "unauthorized use or misuse of Your and Your customers' accounts" and data non-delivery); s3.8 six-month limitation; s4.5 inactive accounts may be deactivated; s5.3/s6.4 renewals and fees non-refundable; s9 default parked page may carry third-party ads "related to ... Your and Your customers' competitors" until nameservers are set; s11.4(i) transfers may be refused "Where You are in default of any obligation towards GDG"; s14.3 registry terms not binding on GDG; s14.5 you indemnify the registry operator within 30 days of demand for claims "arising out of or relating in any way to" your domain registrations.
- The termination-with-account-transfer power exists only in the 2023 Reseller Agreement. The GDG ToU has no such sentence, but s7.3(a) ("cease providing any Service") gives the same practical power.
- Corroboration that the GDG ToU is the registrant-facing registrar paper: GDG's RDAP `/help` (https://rdap.globaldomaingroup.com/help) carries a "Terms of Service" notice pointing at the GDG terms page.

### C4. Dynadot ToU s3.1(b) tells a reselling account holder to say it is "neither a domain name registrar accredited by ICANN nor an authorized reseller of Dynadot's Services" (CONFIRMED; nuance and omission)
- https://www.dynadot.com/terms-of-use (v2026-08-18) s3.1 opens "Prior to reselling a domain name to a third party:" then (a) notify the third party the domain is registered with Dynadot, (b) the quoted sentence, (c) written acceptance of this Agreement, (d) "Any agreement between You and that third party shall provide that the terms of this Agreement shall prevail over the terms of the agreement between You and that third party."
- MISSED: s3.1(a) and (d). (d) would force Mosshatch's customer terms to be subordinate to Dynadot's ToU, which is incompatible with Mosshatch owning its customer contract; (a) discloses Dynadot rather than GDG, which contradicts the Reseller Agreement's "identify GDG".
- Judgment: the clause is written for aftermarket-style resale of an individual name, not program resellers, so it probably does not apply as written; but the Reseller Agreement incorporates the whole Dynadot ToU as "additional terms" with no precedence rule, so the ambiguity is real. The analyst's "conflict, get it in writing" stands.
- Related marketing inconsistency (not in the dossier): the Dec 2025 blog says "To them, you're the registrar" and "Because Dynadot is an ICANN-accredited registrar, we handle all ... compliance". The contract says the reseller must not "represent Yourself as accredited by ICANN" and must name GDG on request. Mosshatch copy must follow the contract, not the blog.

### C5. Dynadot ToU s13.1(b)(c) / GDG ToU s12.1(b)(c) bar making the API "or any data derived out of its use" available to third parties or embedding it "into other applications or third parties" (CONFIRMED verbatim; severity CORRECTED/NUANCED)
- Verbatim match in both documents (Dynadot ToU s13.1; GDG ToU s12.1). Also s13.1(h): use "in a manner determined by Dynadot, in its absolute and sole discretion, as abusive of the API service" (relevant to high-volume availability polling on the Regular tier) and (i): no use of API code "to ... develop, promote, distribute, sell or support any product or service that is competitive with the API" (Mosshatch's adapter is internal, but note the wording). The Reseller Agreement imports the Dynadot ToU, so s13.1 binds a reseller.
- Mitigating primary evidence the analyst did not cite: API landing page (https://www.dynadot.com/domain/api) "can be integrated into scripts, internal tools, websites, or other backend systems"; "Reseller Requests: Our API functions extend to our reseller program"; the reseller blog above. Server-side use by Mosshatch is "use", not "making the API available"; the exposure is the literal "any data derived out of its use ... to third parties" (prices, availability shown to customers). The API changelog (2025-10-09) also says "Search Access Restricted ... for performance and data control reasons", consistent with Dynadot policing derived availability data.
- Net: risk is textual and real; intent points the other way; s3.13 precedence means only a signed written statement removes it. Written waiver remains the right ask.

### C6. Unilateral amendment, suspension and termination; no notice periods (CONFIRMED)
- Reseller Agreement, GDG ToU s1.4/s7.3/s4.5, Dynadot ToU s1.4/s7.3 all use "without specific advance notice" / "sole and absolute discretion". No cure period anywhere.

### C7. Registrant of record is the person whose data Mosshatch supplies; Mosshatch stays liable unless it names the customer within 7 days (CONFIRMED, with a correction on Designated Agent)
- RAA 3.7.7 and 3.7.7.3 (licensee stays "Registered Name Holder of record" and "shall accept liability for harm caused by wrongful use ... unless it discloses the current contact information provided by the licensee and the identity of the licensee within seven (7) days") are unchanged in the Jan 2024 text; the Reseller Agreement and GDG ToU s3.9 extend the same rule to the reseller.
- Note: the 7-day disclosure only removes the wrongful-use liability. GDG ToU s4.2 (all account-use liabilities) and the indemnities remain.
- CORRECTION/MISSED: Dynadot ToU s11.3 reads "You expressly appoint Dynadot as Your Designated Agent ..." (covers the account holder only). GDG ToU s11.3 adds "and Your customer's". If the Dynadot paper governs, Mosshatch's own registration agreement must have each end customer appoint the registrar as Designated Agent and opt out of the 60-day lock, or change-of-registrant will need customer-side approvals. (The API 500 error the analyst cites - "ensure Dynadot is Designated Agent" - points the same way.)
- Dynadot help (https://www.dynadot.com/help/question/reseller-accounts, updated 2023/11/08) itself warns "Often with resellers, the domain you purchase is not owned by you, but is instead owned by the reseller" - a reminder that Mosshatch must actually record the customer as RNH.

### C8. ICANN RAA flow-down citations 3.7.7, 3.12, 3.18 (CORRECTED)
- The analyst cited https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/approved-with-specs-2013-09-17-en (the 2013-09-17 text). ICANN's RAA index (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/accredited-registrars/registrar-accreditation-agreement) says: "2013 Registrar Accreditation Agreement (RAA) - Approved 21 January 2024 ... The 2024 global amendment to existing registrar accreditation agreements is effective 5 April 2024." I fetched the current text (https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/registrar-accreditation-agreement-21jan24-en.htm).
- Unchanged in substance: 1.26 Reseller definition ("participates in Registrar's distribution channel ... collecting registration data about Registered Name Holders, submitting that data to Registrar, or facilitating the entry of the registration agreement"); 3.12 lead-in ("Registrar must enter into written agreements with all of its Resellers"), 3.12.1-3.12.7; 3.7.7 and 3.7.7.3.
- Changed: 3.18.1 now covers "DNS Abuse and Illegal Activity", requires an emailed/webform contact "conspicuously and readily accessible from the home page" and an acknowledgement to the reporter; new 3.18.2 "When Registrar has actionable evidence that a Registered Name ... is being used for DNS Abuse, Registrar must promptly take the appropriate mitigation action(s)"; former 3.18.2/3.18.3 are now 3.18.3/3.18.4. GDG's own abuse page already frames spam/phishing/malware as "a registrar's responsibility to address ... DNS abuse". Practical effect: GDG must act on DNS abuse against Mosshatch customer domains, and its sole-discretion suspension rights (GDG ToU s7.3) are how it does so.
- Verified IANA IDs: https://www.iana.org/assignments/registrar-ids/registrar-ids-1.csv rows `472,Dynadot Inc,Accredited,https://rdap.dynadot.com/` and `3956,Global Domain Group LLC,Accredited,https://rdap.globaldomaingroup.com/`. (IANA also lists ~20 other Dynadot-branded LLC registrar IDs, e.g. 1559, 1637; not relevant to reseller sponsorship.)

### C18. Expired reseller-account domains and auctions (CORRECTED - contract answers it; practice unverified)
- GDG ToU s8.1(b) "You shall have no right to access or to use the domain name" during the 40-day Post-Expiration Period; s8.4 "GDG may auction for sale any domain name that is not renewed within the lesser of the first 30 calendar days of the Post-Expiration Period ..."; s8.5 "GDG may transfer any domain name to another registrant". No reseller carve-out. Dynadot help "What is an Expired Domain Auction?" (updated 2026/06/04): "When a domain is not renewed by the registrant and has passed its grace renewal period, it will go to expired auction." The analyst's "applicability unverified" should read "permitted by contract; practice for reseller accounts unverified". Mosshatch must renew customer domains before expiry and must disclose this in its own terms.

### C19. Data-handling and privacy terms (CONFIRMED absence, expanded)
- Searched both ToUs and the Reseller Agreement for GDPR, controller/processor, standard contractual clauses, cross-border transfer, sub-processor, data processing agreement: zero hits. The only reseller data clause is consent-passing ("notices and obtain consents equivalent to those enumerated under Part II ... from every one of Your customers"). Part II permits processing for "targeted marketing", "disclosure of non-public RDDS/WHOIS/RDAP to third parties", and "to train, build and improve the accuracy of automated processing methods" (GDG ToU Part II s3.3). No breach-notification duty to the reseller (RAA 3.20 covers notice to ICANN only). For EU/UK end customers this is a gap Mosshatch cannot paper over unilaterally. An offline DPA on request is UNVERIFIABLE.
