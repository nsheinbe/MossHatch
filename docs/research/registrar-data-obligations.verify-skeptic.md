## Things the dossier missed (2025-2026 changes and gaps)

1. DNS Abuse Mitigation PDP 1 Initial Report (GNSO, 18 Aug 2026; public comment closed 28 Sep 2026): eight preliminary recommendations to make registrars run "Associated Domain Checks" when they hold actionable DNS Abuse evidence; Preliminary Implementation Guidance 3.1 (under Recommendation 3, Reasonable Investigation) says a registrar "may rely on a Reseller to conduct ADC investigation steps" but "remain[s] responsible for all relevant contractual obligations, as required by 3.12 of the RAA". Board delivery expected Jul 2027 (Sept 2026 scorecard). Sources: https://www.icann.org/en/public-comment/proceeding/dns-abuse-mitigation-pdp-1-initial-report-18-08-2026 and https://gnso.icann.org/sites/default/files/policy/2026/draft/dnsam-pdp-initial-report-18aug26-en.pdf .
2. DNS Abuse PDP 2 (initiated Dec 2025, not yet chartered): "Checks to establish trust for new registrants prior to providing access to high-volume registration tools"; Final Issue Report (4 Dec 2025) gap P1 "Unrestricted Access to APIs to new customers" cites INFERMAL (API access linked to a 401% increase in malicious registrations). This is squarely on Mosshatch's agent/API-driven model and supports the dossier's "velocity limits" idea; expect upstream registrars to add friction earlier than the PDP finishes.
3. ICANN Advisory on RDDS communication web forms (25 May 2026) and the 26 May 2026 announcement (see row 18).
4. Board adoption of all 47 Transfer Policy Review recommendations on 7 Jun 2026 (row 37), including Rec 21 (registrar MAY deny transfer for a name identified as a DNS Abuse source) and a separate CORD Policy; nothing effective yet.
5. RAA 3.20: registrar must notify ICANN within 7 days of "any unauthorized access to or disclosure of registrant account information or registration data" (with details). Not in dossier. A reseller holding registrant data will be bound by a notice SLA shorter than 7 days; the dossier only covers GDPR's 72 hours.
6. Additional Registrar Operations Specification (RAA 3.19): registrar "must allow its customers to use DNSSEC upon request by relaying orders to add, remove or change public key material (DNSKEY or DS)", IPv6 glue where nameserver addresses are offered, IDN rules. A reseller storefront/API must expose DS management and IPv6 glue. Not in the dossier checklist.
7. Logo License Specification / RAA 2.2: registrar may not sublicense ICANN's trademarks to any Reseller without ICANN's written approval, and must notify ICANN of suspected infringement by resellers. Explains why 3.12.1 exists; adds a registrar-side duty.
8. ICANN enforcement is active: first six months of the DNS Abuse rules (5 Apr to 5 Oct 2024) saw 192 investigations, notices of breach to one registry operator and one registrar, 154 cases resolved, 2,700+ names suspended (announcement 8 Nov 2024); ICANN published a complaint how-to guide 20 Nov 2025.
9. Retention framing (row 19): RAA 3.4.2 "Term of this Agreement + 2 years" is a whole-agreement clock; the RDP FAQ says 15 months replaces two years for the defined RDP data set.
10. RDRS: Board (30 Oct 2025) extended operations to Dec 2027; SSAD Supplemental Recommendations are heading to public comment (Oct 2026 estimate) with Board delivery Mar 2027 (Sept 2026 scorecard). Relevant to future disclosure obligations, not to Phase 0 build.
11. 2026 Base Registry Agreement (Board 12 Mar 2026) applies only to the 2026 new gTLD round; no change to RAA or to .com/.dev/.app/.studio agreements. No effect on Phase 0.
12. ERRP / EDDP Policy Status Report went to public comment 15 May to 30 Jun 2025; I did not find its outcome. If the GNSO moves to change ERRP, the reseller-website clauses (4.1.2, 4.2.3) could change.
13. If Mosshatch or an operator ever registers names as Registered Name Holder on behalf of a customer, that is a "Proxy Service" under the P/P Spec definitions; dossier design implication 1 (Account Holder vs RNH) should say so.

## Bottom line

The five assigned re-derivations hold: 15-day verification rule (Accuracy Spec s1-s5, text unchanged since 2013), RAA 3.12 list (seven items + enforcement sentence, one cosmetic change since 2013), RDP effective 21 Aug 2025 (revised 12 May 2026, only s3.8/3.9/10.7 added), WHOIS sunset 28 Jan 2025 (computed and announced), and DNS Abuse amendment scope (only 3.18 restated, effective 5 Apr 2024, DNS Abuse = five types, mitigation duty only on actionable evidence). No RAA global amendment after 5 Apr 2024 exists as of 2026-09-29. Corrections: retention framing (row 19), Transfer Policy status (row 37), and minor wording (rows 10, 15, 18). The largest gaps are the 2026 DNS Abuse PDP work aimed at API-driven bulk registration, RAA 3.20, and the DNSSEC/IPv6/IDN specification.
