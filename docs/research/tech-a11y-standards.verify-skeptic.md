## Things I could not re-derive (left as unverifiable)
- That domain-name registration is an EAA "e-commerce service": the text supports it for consumer purchases through a website, but I found no Commission guidance, FAQ or case that names domains. (Same as the analyst.)
- Whether v4.1.1 has been cited in the OJ after 7 Sep 2026 (OJ not searchable in this session; only negative evidence from AccessibleEU news up to 24 Sep and the Commission harmonised-standards page).
- Member State transposition outside DE and IE, including whether Ireland's S.I. 636/2023 has been amended.
- Colour-vision simulations, real-device AT behaviour, and OS hold-duration settings (not attempted).

## Missed by the analyst (important or recent)
1. Recommendation 2003/361/EC mechanics (two consecutive accounting periods; AWU headcount counting owner-managers; partner/linked aggregation; VAT-exclusive turnover; bona fide estimate for new firms). This changes the "exemption ends when it grows" advice and the A-04 DECISIONS.md entry.
2. 2.5.8 "Equivalent" needs "the same page" (see row 30).
3. Spain: Royal Decree 707/2026 on cognitive accessibility, in force 2 Jan 2027, reportedly obliging "businesses" to provide plain-language/Easy Read information (AccessibleEU 14 Sep 2026: https://accessible-eu-centre.ec.europa.eu/content-corner/news/spain-introduces-groundbreaking-cognitive-accessibility-regulation-2026-09-14_en). Only the AccessibleEU summary was read; scope for an online domain shop unknown. Relevant if Spain is a target market.
4. EN 301 549 v4.1.1 national dates (doa 30 Nov 2026, dop/e 31 May 2027, dow 31 May 2028) and the EAA-specific Annex ZB / clause A.2 conformity mapping; AccessibleEU frames v4.1.1 as adding six new (A/AA) WCAG 2.2 requirements.
5. WCAG 2.2 AAA 2.4.13 Focus Appearance and 2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced) are not in the A-02 AAA target list; the 2px/2px/2px ring probably satisfies 2.4.13 but nobody measured it.
6. Red flash threshold is not addressed (only the general flash threshold).
7. Commission v Bulgaria (C-646/24) shows transposition gaps exist; check target Member States' status individually.
8. The eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y / ESLint 10 mismatch is a real install blocker, not just "check".
9. Checklist A-51 wording: assert target-size appears in the UNION of passes, violations, incomplete and inapplicable (a route with no undersized or any targets would otherwise fail the CI assertion spuriously).
10. WebAIM regional split (JAWS 72.5% vs NVDA 15.6% in North America; NVDA far ahead in Asia, Africa/Middle East, South America; Europe about even): if the customer base is global, NVDA weight is higher than the 32.9% headline.
11. Minor US context: Missouri's anti-abusive-website-suit law (operative 28 Aug 2026) and the NFB challenge to Title II deadline extensions (June 2026) - neither changes Title III exposure for an out-of-state seller.
12. Summary line "coloured text needs alpha 0.80 or more (... Pool 0.91)" is internally inconsistent: 0.80 does not make Pool text pass (needs 0.907-0.915). The body (A-40) handles it by forbidding Pool text on glass, so only the summary wording needs fixing.

## Overall
The dossier is accurate on every numeric, date and quotation I re-checked; no claim was refuted. Two substantive corrections (microenterprise loss-of-status rule; 2.5.8 "same page") and one emphasis correction (EN 301 549 v3.2.1 is not visibly cited under the EAA in any Commission listing I could open). Confidence: high on facts, medium on the legal-reading items which remain lawyer questions.
