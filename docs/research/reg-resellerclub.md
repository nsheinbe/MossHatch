# ResellerClub (LogicBoxes / Newfold Digital): registrar dossier for Mosshatch Phase 0

Research date and access date for every source below: **2026-09-29**. Analyst scope: research only, no code written.

## TL;DR

1. **Role: FALLBACK, not primary.** Reselling under our own brand, price and checkout through the HTTP API is what the LogicBoxes reseller contract and docs describe, but I could not read ResellerClub's own contract, price pages or KB (Cloudflare challenge that needs a host our egress policy blocks). ResellerClub-specific prices and terms are therefore **unverified**.
2. **Where the evidence comes from:** LogicBoxes' own demo-registrar KB and default agreements (`demo.myorderbox.com`, same platform, "Demo Registrar" branding), WHMCS docs (last modified 2026-08-04), the FOSSBilling adapter source, ICANN/IANA/RDAP and registry pages.
3. **API maps to all 13 RegistrarAdapter methods** (7 yes, 6 partial) with gaps: no webhooks (poll `actions/search-current`), no idempotency key, DNS is provider-hosted but per-record CRUD keyed by (host, value) with no record IDs and no CAA, no raw EPP status codes, outbound transfers cannot be approved through the API.
4. **Hard operational blocker to plan for:** API calls are only accepted from **at most 3 whitelisted single IPs** (no ranges, 30-60 min to apply). Vercel functions need Static IPs (Pro/Enterprise, $100/month per project, one IP pair per region).
5. **Rate limits are unpublished.** "Reasonable limit" exceeded means a **24-hour IP block**; repeated abuse means permanent account block. Bulk availability checks on registered names and creating customers with no orders are named as abuse.
6. **Money model:** prepaid Advance Account. Negative balance: termination after 10 days, and after 30 days the provider "may take ownership of all Orders". Price changes on 90 days' email notice; suspension and freeze rights are broad and without notice.
7. **Lifecycle (vendor KB, .com/.app/.dev):** 4-day money-back window, 30-day (.com) or 40-day (.app/.dev/Donuts) renewal grace, 30-day redemption (up to 5 working days to restore), 5-day transfer-in, 60-day ICANN locks. Expired names are parked on a commercial page and may be monetised or transferred.
8. **Prices for all six TLDs are unverified** (ResellerClub pricing pages unreadable; the authoritative source is `products/reseller-cost-price.json` after signup). Documented gotchas: `.ai` 2-year only, `.dev`/`.app` need `tnc=y` plus HTTPS-only preload, `.io` and `.ai` are missing from the vendor KB's product tables.
9. **WHOIS privacy is a paid add-on** (free redaction only for EEA-contact "GDPR Protection"); the vendor KB conflicts on whether `.ai` supports privacy at all.

### 1.2 Clauses that answer "may we resell under our own brand, price and checkout via the API?"

1. **Customers and sub-resellers are contemplated.** Master Agreement 4(5): "Contractee agrees that OrderBox Products under this agreement may be made available to Customers, and Lower Tier Sub-Resellers only after they enter into a legally binding agreement which is no less protective of LogicBoxes than this Agreement and which contains the requirements contained herein applicable to the Contractee." Master 4(1): "The Contractee shall be responsible for providing customer service, billing support, and technical support to their Lower Tier Sub-Resellers & Customers." (https://demo.myorderbox.com/servlet/ViewAgreementServlet?requestfor=resellermasteragreement&pgaction=back)
2. **API/own storefront is an intended channel.** Reseller startup guide: "Along with your Reseller account, you get a comprehensive HTTP API. All Resellers have API access to the system. Every functionality of the system is available as an API call." and "Build your own Control Panels using our HTTP API" (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq692.html).
3. **Own price.** Domain Registration Setup Guide step: "Set your Selling Price for different TLDs that you are selling." (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq971.html). Exception: "You cannot set the Selling Price for Premium Domains ... You as a Reseller, your Sub-Reseller(s) and the end Customer will be charged the same Price for a Premium Domain." (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/1953)
4. **Own brand.** "Every e-mail sent to your Sub-Reseller/Customer, however, is sent transparently from YOUR e-mail address. Demo Registrar Name/Address is not mentioned anywhere in this process." (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq334.html). The .COM/.NET port-43 WHOIS shows "Registration Service Provided by (your Brand Name will be displayed here)", toggleable (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq453.html).
5. **Ordering on the customer's behalf.** `domains/register.json` requires `customer-id` and contact IDs of the registrant (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/752). Reseller Domain Extension 2(2): "The Reseller will not make any changes to any information associated with the Domain Order without explicit authorization from the Registrant of that Domain Order."
6. **ICANN flow-down.** RAA 3.12: "Registrar must enter into written agreements with all of its Resellers that enable Registrar to comply with and perform all of its obligations under this Agreement." 3.12.2: reseller registration agreements must "include all registration agreement provisions and notices required by the ICANN Registrar Accreditation Agreement and any ICANN Consensus Policies, and shall identify the sponsoring registrar". 3.12.1: resellers must not display the ICANN or accredited-registrar logo. 3.12.5: link customers to ICANN's registrant educational page. 3.12.7: publish the Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities Specification.

### 4.1 Making `register` safely retryable (no idempotency key exists)

The docs define no idempotency key, no client request id, and no statement about duplicate registrations. What the API does give us: order lookup by domain name, an action queue we can poll, and an `available.json` status of `regthroughus`. A safe pattern (design advice built on those documented endpoints, not a vendor guarantee):

1. Persist an intent row (UUID, name, years, contact snapshot, wholesale price at quote time, state) in Neon **before** any call; treat the UUID as our idempotency key on our side only.
2. Call `available.json` immediately before `register.json`; `regthroughus` means the name is already in our reseller account.
3. On timeout, 5xx, HTML body, unknown JSON shape or "outcome unknown": **do not resend.** Reconcile in this order: `GET domains/orderid.json?domain-name=`, then `GET domains/details-by-name.json`, then `GET actions/search-current.json` / `search-archived.json` by `order-id`. The action may be queued ("Order Locked in Processing"), not failed.
4. Only retry after all three show nothing, and keep the same intent row.
5. `renew.json` requires the current `exp-date`, so re-reading `details.json` before a renew retry naturally detects an already-applied renewal (inference).
6. Keep the Advance Account balance above the sum of pending wholesale costs, monitor `billing/reseller-balance.json` and the emailed funds threshold; a low balance turns retries into duplicate queued orders.
7. Validate in the demo account: what `register.json` returns for an already-registered name, a re-sent identical request, and `invoice-option=NoInvoice` (whether it debits our Advance Account as Appendix C implies).

## 9. RED FLAGS

Contract (all from the LogicBoxes default templates at `demo.myorderbox.com`; ResellerClub's live copy unverified):

1. **Unilateral price changes.** Master 6(5): "LogicBoxes reserves the right to change pricing, minimum order levels, and discounts, at any time, by providing a 90 day advance notice to the Contractee via email." Our only remedy is 8(4): terminate a product extension "within 30 days of such revision".
2. **Unilateral term changes.** Master 15(2): revisions "will be binding and effective upon notification to the Contractee via email or within the Contractee Control Panel"; our remedy is termination.
3. **Suspension/freeze without notice.** Master 6(8): LogicBoxes and Service Providers may "without notice or refund, access, freeze, delete, suspend, deny, cancel, modify ... any Order" on suspected breach; 6(7) allows freezing OrderBox without notice; Appendix A 1(11) suspend API access on suspected misuse at "ABSOLUTE and UNFETTERED SOLE DISCRETION". Reseller Extension 3 repeats this for Domain Orders.
4. **Balance default seizes domains.** Appendix C 1(5): "If the Contractee does not remedy a negative balance in their account within 10 days, LogicBoxes has the right to terminate this agreement with immediate effect and without any notice." 1(6): "If a negative balance in the Contractee's Advance Account is not cured within 30 days, LogicBoxes may take ownership of all Orders belonging to Contractee in the OrderBox Database." Card chargeback on a top-up: suspend access, terminate, and "Transfer all Orders placed by the Lower Tier Sub-Resellers or Customers of the Contractee to any other party" (Appendix C 2(5)(4)). Locked funds during 5-10 day transfers reduce headroom.
5. **No refund of used balance.** Appendix C 4(4): "LogicBoxes will not refund any amount that has already been debited to the Contractee's Advance Account under any circumstances, unless such debit is found to be erroneous." Refund of unused balance takes "up to 14 Business Days" where "Business Day" excludes "Public Holidays in India".
6. **Expired customer names can be monetised or moved.** Master 9(5) and the registrant agreement's Expired Domain Transfer (quoted in section 6); parking-page redirect for expired/suspended orders (Master 6(9)); nameservers switched to parking on expiry. Directly relevant to a product that keeps a project's secrets and DNS tied to the domain.
7. **Privacy can be switched off by the provider** at its "sole, unfettered discretion" (Reseller Extension Appendix A).
8. **Jurisdiction.** Master 22-23: Singapore law, SIAC arbitration seated in Singapore; template counterparty is "PDR Ltd., a Cayman Islands Limited company". Cost and friction for a small company (opinion).
9. **Indemnity flow-down.** Extension 2(1): the reseller "shall indemnify Parent of, and shall be responsible for any liability resulting from Registrants' noncompliance".

API/operational:

10. **Cloudflare in front of docs, console and API** (section 0): our egress IP got "Sorry, you have been blocked" from `test.httpapi.com`; integrators see WAF/rate-limit pages returned as 2xx (FOSSBilling comment "see #4220 for the same class of issue").
11. **IP allow-list of at most 3 single IPs, 30-60 minutes to apply**, plus shared Static IP pairs on Vercel (a noisy neighbour on a shared IP could get the pair blocked for 24 h: inference).
12. **Opaque limits, 24-hour IP ban, permanent account block** (section 4); our per-user-customer design and creature-driven bulk availability checks sit close to the two named abuse patterns.
13. **Inconsistent API surface** (from integrator code): booleans as bare text with varying case ("the registrar isn't consistent about formatting", issue #2939), scalar responses on some endpoints, three error shapes, response-key casing not guaranteed. Doc typo: the transfer article lists `auth-key` where every example uses `api-key` (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/758).
14. **Docs staleness.** No `.ai`, `.io`, `.dev`, `.app` rows in the product-key table; TLD overview lacks `.ai`/`.io`; `.ai` privacy conflict; transfer article still describes admin-contact approval after the 2018 FOA change; ResellerClub's own KB appears to have moved (search results showed `resellerclub.webpropanel.com/kb/...` and `www.resellerclub.com/help/...`), so parameter drift versus this KB cannot be excluded (unverified).
15. **Contacts are immutable** (since Dec 2016): every change creates a new contact object, and the customer-without-orders abuse pattern makes clutter risky.
16. **Manual, slow redemption** (up to 5 working days) and a deletion fee even in the money-back window.
17. **Outages / status:** no status page reachable and no incident history found; I have no evidence either way (unverified).
18. **Ownership context:** `resellerclub.com` RDAP shows registrant contact `corpdomains@newfold.com`, last changed 2026-08-11; I found no ownership-change news but could not search for it.

## 10. RECOMMENDATION

**Fallback.** ResellerClub is a plausible second provider: its reseller model is exactly "wholesale into a prepaid balance, resell under our own brand and price", the HTTP API maps to all thirteen RegistrarAdapter methods (seven fully, and `quote`, `getDomain`, `listRecords`, `upsertRecord`, `getTransfer`, `startTransferOut` only partially), DNS hosting is included, a Test URL plus a demo reseller account exist, and the contract expressly contemplates customers, own pricing and our own registrant agreement. It should not be primary on today's evidence because (1) every ResellerClub-specific fact that decides economics and risk (cost prices for the six TLDs, deposit and slab tiers, KYC, the live agreements, current KB) was unreadable behind a Cloudflare challenge that our egress policy prevents us from passing, (2) the API imposes real engineering costs on our stack: a mandatory fixed egress (at most three single IPs, so Vercel Static IPs at $100/month per project), opaque limits with a 24-hour IP ban, no webhooks, no idempotency key and an inconsistent error surface, and (3) the contract is hostile in ways that matter to a product whose value is keeping a domain alive (90-day unilateral price changes, no-notice freezes, domain seizure on a 30-day negative balance, post-expiry monetisation or transfer of names). `.ai` and `.io` coverage, privacy on `.ai`, and duplicate-register behaviour are undocumented in the sources I could read. Promote it only after a human (a) opens the demo and live reseller accounts and downloads `products/reseller-cost-price.json` for `.com .ai .dev .io .app .studio`, (b) reads ResellerClub's own agreements via Help > Legal Agreements or `commons/legal-agreements`, and (c) proves in the demo account: duplicate and retried `register.json`, `NoInvoice` debiting, `.ai` 2-year flow, DNS upsert semantics and 3-IP allow-listing from the intended egress.
