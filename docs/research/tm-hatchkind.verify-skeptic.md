# Skeptic verification: tm-hatchkind (HATCHKIND / HATCH knockout and hatchkind.com share host)

Verifier lens: skeptic. Date of run: 2026-09-29, roughly 20:24 to 20:50 UTC. All re-fetches were done by me from primary sources; the analyst's quotes and URLs were not trusted. Working files: `research/vsk-hk/` (raw JSON/HTML/TXT plus my scripts `uspto.js`, `tmv.js`, `euipo.js`).

Bottom line: the load-bearing facts hold. The two lookups that matter most (no HATCHKIND mark; HATCH crowding counts) reproduce essentially exactly. I found five corrections and two gaps:

1. ICANN 60-day rule is stronger than written: for .com it is a registry hard block, not just a registrar option.
2. Gmail's baseline sender rules (2024-02-01) apply to all senders, not only the 5,000+/day tier.
3. "Firefox is still considering Related Origin Requests" is a January 2026 snapshot and looks stale (Mozilla standards-position issue closed 2026-03-11 labelled "position: positive"; implementation not verified).
4. One count (63 HATCH-compounds) does not reproduce (60 by my tokenization).
5. The structured summary's source_url for the open-web claim (an Amazon store page) is not where the quote came from. WebSearch could not be re-run by me (session budget of 200 exhausted), so that claim stays unverifiable.
6. Gap: TMview freshness varies a lot by office (CNIPA 2026-09-03, India and eight others 2026-05, Jordan 2022-07, Russia 2022-02).
7. Gap: class-number arithmetic ("class 45 thinnest") is not a legal risk measure (15 U.S.C. 1112).

## 8. Things the analyst missed or under-weighted

1. `hatch` and `hatchling` are widely used PyPI packages (PyPA's Hatch project manager and build backend, hatch 1.18.1 and hatchling 1.32.4 at fetch time; homepage hatch.pypa.io, source github.com/pypa/hatch), and `hatch` is also an existing npm package. For a developer- and CLI-facing product this is a real name-collision and search-noise point that the dossier's "Hatch" web list (sleep devices, Hatch Apps, Hatch AI, Hatch Kids, Hatchlings) omits. It concerns HATCH alone, not HATCHKIND.
2. 15 U.S.C. 1112: classification is "for convenience of Patent and Trademark Office administration, but not to limit or extend the applicant's or registrant's rights". "Class 45 is the least crowded" is arithmetic only; confusion is judged on relatedness of goods and services, not class number. TMview shows 34 live exact-HATCH marks in class 9, 49 in 35, 23 in 36, 51 in 42 and 7 in 45, and HATCH LABS (class 45) plus Hatch Ltd. EM/UK 2026 filings (classes 42 and 45) sit right next to registrar and software services.
