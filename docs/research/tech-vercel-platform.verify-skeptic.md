# Skeptic verification: tech-vercel-platform (Mosshatch Phase 0)

Verifier date: 2026-09-29. Lens: skeptic. All evidence re-fetched by me with curl (browser UA, `Accept: text/markdown` for Vercel docs, proxy as pre-configured, TLS verification on). Working copies in `research/skv/` (docs `*.txt`, changelog atom `atom.xml`, status pages `hist*.html` and `inc/*.html`, `meta.json`, `codes.txt`).

Tool notes: WebSearch is out of budget (200/200), so recent-change hunting was done by scanning the Vercel changelog atom feed (https://vercel.com/atom, 1,618 entries, same as the analyst) and by re-opening each cited page. A GitHub API call (vercel/workflow releases) returned 403 "not enabled for this session"; not worked around. Two Vercel pages (changelog `spend-management-now-pauses-production-deployments-by-default`, and one retry of `/docs/security/compliance`) briefly returned a Vercel "challenge" 403; the compliance page succeeded on a plain retry, the changelog page was replaced by the atom feed content.

## Overall verdict

Core numbers and policy claims re-derive cleanly (Hobby non-commercial, Pro $20 + 1 seat + $20 credit, 300/800/1800 s durations, 4.5 MB bodies, 2 GB/4 GB memory, cron 100/project + per-minute on Pro + best-effort/duplicate/no-retry, Node 20 cutoff 2026-10-01, Static IPs $100/month, logs 1 day, Drains $0.50/GB, WAF 40/40 with per-region counters, incident counts 133/3/28/81, iad1 incident quotes). Three things are stale or unresolved and change advice:

1. CORRECTED: "Enforce Sensitive Environment Variables" team policy is DEPRECATED as of 2026-08-24 (Sensitive toggle replaced by Config/Secret types; new policy "Separate Production Secret Values"). The dossier's checklist item and design implication #6 tell the team to enable the deprecated policy.
2. CORRECTED/RESOLVED: the Pro CDN "1M vs 10M" conflict is explained by Flat Rate CDN (GA for Pro on 2026-09-08, default for new Pro teams: 1M requests + 1 TB included; 10M is the legacy on-demand allowance on the pricing page). The analyst missed Flat Rate CDN entirely.
3. NEW OPTION: since 2026-09-09 Vercel Authentication can protect production deployments for free on every plan ("All Deployments"). Dossier's "Standard Protection protects previews, not production" is still true for Standard, but the dossier does not mention the new free option or its interaction with cron/webhooks.

## Important facts the analyst missed (see structured output `missed`)

- Terms s3: Hobby AND trial Pro plans grant Vercel a license to use Your Content for AI Model Training and share it with third parties; paid Pro is off by default (opt-in); opt-out via team setting or Enterprise. Anything deployed during a Pro trial or on Hobby (code, and any data in it) is in scope.
- Terms s9.2 (PCI): "Vercel is not a payment processor"; customer is responsible for PCI DSS and "agree[s] to comply with Vercel's Documentation on appropriate implementation of the Services for processing payments" (compliance page recommends a processor-hosted iframe/redirect). Stronger customer-side obligation than the dossier's "Vercel supports PCI" line.
- Legal-terms update 2026-06-04: customer responsible for actions (and costs) of AI Functionality and any Third-Party Tool granted account access (CI, agents, scripts); Vercel may charge accrued fees ahead of the cycle for unusual usage (partial invoices); OFAC representation.
- Threshold billing (partial invoices mid-cycle once on-demand usage hits a threshold) enabled for Pro on 2026-06-10.
- Flat Rate CDN GA (2026-09-08) and its fair-use carve-outs.
- Free "All Deployments" Vercel Authentication for production (2026-09-09) and the Config/Secret env-var change (2026-08-24) (both above).
- Workflow blog (2026-08-27) states v5 is beta and v6 is coming.
- Vercel KMS incident 2026-09-18 and a 2026-09-28 Edge-runtime incident (already in dossier).
