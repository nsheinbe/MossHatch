## Important things the analyst missed (or under-stated)

1. AWS .ai: the AWS developer guide page for .ai says "Registration: Two to ten years. Renewal: Two to nine years." and "Privacy protection: Not supported." So the $137/year figure means at least $274 due at registration, and the dossier's per-year comparison (and the 20-domain quota planning) should use that. eNom's .ai ($111) and Internet.bs's ($92.40) period basis is also unstated. (docs.aws.amazon.com/Route53/latest/DeveloperGuide/ai.html)
2. AWS agreement risk terms beyond 3.12: 3.12.8 (liability for customer's use), 2.4 (indemnity to AWS, the Registrar, the Registry), 2.2.2 (AWS may reject any registration), 3.10.5 (you stay bound while any domain remains in your account, even if RDAP names a third party), 3.1.1 (a third-party WhoisProxy.com privacy/proxy is switched on automatically, "except where prohibited"), 3.11.4 (60-day transfer restrictions), AWS Service Terms 13.1 (no hosted zone for a domain you do not own or have authority over - reseller needs the customer's authority). AWS registrar of record is Amazon Registrar, Inc. or other accredited registrars (2.1).
3. AWS hosted-zone quota is 500/account by default, and each registered domain creates a hosted zone that costs $0.50/month for the first 25 ($0.10 after; deleted within 12 h is free).
4. GoDaddy: the v3 API spec lacks renewal/transfer/contact endpoints, so a full lifecycle needs v1/v2, whose sso-key auth is "scheduled for deprecation in 2026". Standard .com renewal outside auto-renew is $22.99 on the public v3 table versus $10.69 reseller wholesale, so only the reseller plan (whose terms are unread) has usable economics. GoDaddy's statement of support lists "Setting prices for the reseller's products" under NOT supported (support scope, not a prohibition).
5. Cloudflare April 30, 2026 post ("Agents can now create Cloudflare accounts, buy domains, and deploy", blog.cloudflare.com/agents-stripe-projects/): a protocol co-designed with Stripe Projects lets an agent/platform provision a Cloudflare account and register a domain for a signed-in user. It is account provisioning for the end user (the user must accept Cloudflare's ToS), not resale, so it does not change "not viable as a supply route", but it is a competing distribution path worth knowing about.
6. Cloudflare DRA 1.3 makes the account holder the holder of record and liable for third-party licensees, so even if a reseller product ships, the current DRA model is "you are the registrant", not "your customer is".
7. eNom: Points are non-refundable, a 7-year records-retention duty applies, and you cannot use the API test environment without first buying the reseller account; the Join Today link goes to qas.enom.com (unexplained host, curiosity only). eNom's sale price on .dev/.app/.io has no stated term; if it is registration-only, renewal cost is the regular price (.dev $17, .app $21, .io $60 at Essential).
8. Margin reality check: eNom Essential-tier wholesale for .io ($60) exceeds Vercel's public retail renewal ($46) and Internet.bs's list ($49.98); eNom .studio ($42) exceeds Vercel retail ($21.99 first year / $36). Only the Enterprise tier ($100k/yr) brings eNom near those numbers, so a launch-tier Mosshatch would have thin or negative margin on .io/.studio at typical retail parity.
9. Internet.bs failure scope (see row 20): not specific to the three target TLDs.
10. Not checkable from here (no search): whether any news after 2026-09-16 changes Cloudflare RaaS, GoDaddy's reseller plan, or Squarespace terms; .io ccTLD status (rdap.nic.io blocked, IANA record still 2023-01-18); ICANN's per-transaction fee amount.

## Bottom line for the lens

The analyst's headline ranking (eNom, AWS, GoDaddy reseller strongest; Squarespace gated; Cloudflare/Vercel/Hover not viable; Spaceship/Epik/Internet.bs unevaluated) survives every re-check I could make. The corrections are second-order: AWS .io was not repriced on 2026-07-01, AWS .ai has a 2-year minimum and no privacy, Internet.bs's .dev/.app/.studio errors are a broad test-host limitation rather than TLD-specific, and "lowest sampled prices" for Internet.bs holds only for four of six TLDs. Unresolved and load-bearing: GoDaddy API/Reseller terms text, Squarespace and Internet.bs terms, Spaceship and Epik entirely.
