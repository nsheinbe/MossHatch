## Detail

### 1-3. .com (re-derived from primary documents)
- SEC EDGAR, Verisign Form 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-06-30 (accession 0001014473-26-000028, filed 2026-07-23). Text: "we renewed the .com Registry Agreement with ICANN, pursuant to which we will remain the sole registry operator for the .com registry through November 30, 2030. Under the .com Registry Agreement, we are permitted to increase the price of a .com domain name registration by up to 7% in each of the final four years of each six-year period. The current such six-year period began on October 26, 2024. We increased the annual registry-level wholesale fee ... from $9.59 to $10.26 effective September 1, 2024. On April 23, 2026, we announced that we will increase the annual registry-level wholesale fee for each new and renewal .com domain name registration from $10.26 to $10.97 effective November 1, 2026."
- Look for changes: I listed every Verisign SEC filing in 2026 via data.sec.gov submissions JSON (through 2026-09-23). 8-Ks: 2026-02-05, 04-23, 05-21 (5.02/5.07), 06-26 (x2, debt), 07-23 (buyback), 08-27 (8.01). The 08-27 8-K is a .NET increase ($10.91 to $12.00 effective 2027-03-01); nothing new for .com. No later .com price notice, no cap change. Tracker (tldpricechanges.com/tld/com, /upcoming) also shows $10.26 now, $10.97 on 2026-11-01, no later step.
- ICANN .com RA page: operator VeriSign, Inc., Agreement Date 1 December 2024.
- RA text (itp.cdn.icann.org com-agreement-html-01-12-2024): 7.3(d)(i) Maximum Price US$10.26; 7.3(d)(ii) 1.07 x smaller of (prior Maximum Price, highest price charged) "in each Pricing Year of the final four Pricing Years of every six year period, with the first six year period beginning on October 26, 2018"; "Pricing Year" = October 26 to October 25; 7.3(f) "no less than six months prior notice ... and shall continue to offer for periods of up to ten years new and renewal domain name registrations fixed at the price in effect at the time such offer is accepted"; 7.3(g) Maximum Price excludes the ICANN Variable Registry-Level Fee.
- Arithmetic: 10.26 x 1.07 = 10.978 (Y3 cap; Verisign chose 10.97, +6.92%); from 10.97: 11.738, 12.560, 13.439. The analyst's 11.74 / 12.56 / 13.44 are right. Cycle positions: Y3 starts 2026-10-26 (this increase), Y4 2027-10-26, Y5 2028-10-26, Y6 2029-10-26. Six-month notice check: announced 2026-04-23, effective 2026-11-01.
- Omission (not an error in the numbers): 7.3(d)(iii) is a second route to a cost pass-through increase of up to 7% for a new Consensus Policy or documented extraordinary expense, available in any Pricing Year in which (ii) is not used. So "no increase possible in years 1-2 of a cycle" is not strictly true. Low practical risk, but the "ceilings" table should not be read as absolute.
- Also 7.2(d): ICANN may CPI-adjust the registry-level $0.25 each December; the "$0.25" is a floor, not necessarily current. It sits inside the wholesale price anyway.

### 4-6. ICANN per-transaction fee
- ICANN announcement 2025-07-21 (FY26): registrars voted 9 June to 11 July 2025 and approved; "Transaction-Based Fees: $0.20 per transaction is assessed on each annual increment of an add, renew, or transfer transaction that has survived a related add or auto-renew grace period." Per-registrar variable fee: $950,000 per quarter split among registrars; annual accreditation $4,000.
- ICANN FY27 Operating Plan and Budget (May 2026 publication): "ICANN does not intend to raise fees in FY27"; appendix 8.1 "The fees below reflect the same rates as in the FY26 budget and forecast"; 3.2 "If approved, these fees will be billed at $0.20 per transaction." The Board adopted the FY27-31 plans and FY27 OP&B at its 3 May 2026 meeting (ICANN announcement dated 2026-05-11); plans take effect 2026-07-01 absent Empowered Community rejection.
- I paged through ICANN's announcements listing (pages 1-4, covering roughly 2026-02-28 to 2026-09-24). No "Registrars approve fees for FY2027" item exists, unlike the 2025-07-21 FY26 item. So I could not find the FY27 vote result; treat ratification as unverified. New nuance from the budget doc: "If approval is not obtained for these Variable Accreditation Fees, registry operators shall pay ICANN for such fees and may pass through these fees to registrars as contemplated by Section 6.3 of the Base RA." So the $0.20 could reach Mosshatch either as a registrar-level ICANN charge or folded into registry wholesale; the amount is the same.
- Pass-through: ICANN registrar financial-considerations page: "Transaction-based gTLD fee (quarterly). This fee is a flat fee charged for each new registration, renewal or transfer. This fee can be billed by the registrar separately on its invoice to the registrant, but is paid by the registrar to ICANN." (That page dates from 2012 but the sentence stands.)
- Cross-check: Cloudflare mirror .com 10.46 = 10.26 + 0.20; after 2026-11-01 the at-cost floor is 11.17 (+0.71).
- ccTLDs carry no such fee: consistent (no ICANN contract), and Cloudflare .ai = $80.00 exactly.

### 15. "ccTLDs are not bound by ICANN consensus policies"
- ICANN Consensus Policies page: "ICANN's agreements with accredited registrars and with gTLD registry operators require compliance with various specifically stated procedures, 'consensus policies', and temporary policies." The binding mechanism is contractual (RAA, registry agreements), and ccTLD managers sign neither.
- ICANN Bylaws (icann.org/resources/pages/governance/bylaws-en): 1.1(a)(i) ICANN coordinates "the development and implementation of policies concerning the registration of second-level domain names in generic top-level domains" and "with respect to gTLD registrars and registries"; 11.1 GNSO is "responsible for developing ... substantive policies relating to generic top-level domains"; 10.1 "Policies that apply to ccNSO members by virtue of their membership are only those policies developed according to Section 10.4(j) and Section 10.4(k)... Adherence to the results of these [other] activities will be voluntary."
- ccTLD side: nic.io terms/rules contain no reference to ICANN, UDRP or consensus policies (grep clean); nic.ai FAQ says the registry "adheres to" the UDRP (voluntary adoption of one ICANN policy, so "not bound" is not the same as "no overlap").
- Not verifiable: the operational claim that ICANN-style 60-day locks, RGP, ERRP do not exist at .ai/.io. Registry rules are unpublished. Cloudflare docs speak of "ICANN rules prohibit transfers within 60 days" generically; Dynadot says "Each registry sets its own policies. Some TLDs don't have a 60-day lock." .ai now runs on Identity Digital's platform with base-RA-aligned WHOIS (DNW 2025-01-23), so gTLD-like behavior is plausible. Keep the dossier's advice to apply one conservative policy to all six TLDs.

### 17. Base RA notice terms (.dev/.app/.studio)
- .dev base RA (16 Oct 2014) 2.10(a): 30 days' notice for initial registrations; 2.10(b): 180 days for renewals, except that 30 days suffices "if the resulting price is less than or equal to ... a price for which Registry Operator provided a notice pursuant to the first sentence of this Section 2.10(b) within the twelve (12) month period preceding". 1-10 year purchase at the current price. 2.10(c) uniform renewal pricing with the disclosed-higher-renewal-price exception. 6.1 registry-level $0.25 only above 50,000 transactions per quarter. All as quoted, but the carve-out is missing from the dossier.
- Caveat: ICANN's listing for .dev, .app and .studio shows "Global Amendments" and a "Renewal Notice" (contents not retrievable), and ICANN says the current Base gTLD RA was approved by its Board on 21 January 2024. The dossier quotes the original 2014 text; the operative renewed terms are unchecked.

## Things the analyst missed or should tighten
1. FY27 budget note: if registrars do not ratify the variable fees, registries pay and may pass them through (Base RA 6.3), so the $0.20 can show up inside wholesale instead of as a separate line. Amount unchanged.
2. ICANN Board adopted the FY27 OP&B on 3 May 2026 (announcement 2026-05-11); FY27 registrar-fee vote not found in ICANN's announcements (the FY26 vote announcement existed by 2025-07-21).
3. ICANN Registration Data Policy update 2026-05-12: new 24-hour response requirement for urgent requests for non-public gTLD registration data, not effective until a law-enforcement authentication mechanism exists. Relevant to Mosshatch's registrant-data obligations; the dossier only lists the 2025-08-21 effective date.
4. RAA: the FY27 budget says the "most recent version of the RAA was approved in January 2024" (2013 RAA as amended). The dossier's reseller-flow-down checklist cites the 2013 RAA text only; check the 2024 amendments (DNS abuse) before finalising obligations. Not verified by me beyond that sentence.
5. ICANN Board approved the 2026 Base RA on 2026-03-12 for the 2026 round (closed 2026-08-12, more than 1,600 applications). It governs new applicants; whether .dev/.app/.studio migrate to it is unknown.
6. .com RA 7.3(d)(iii) cost pass-through path and 7.2(d) CPI adjustment of registry fees (see above).
7. Verisign announced a .net increase ($10.91 to $12.00, effective 2027-03-01) on 2026-08-27 (8-K). Not one of the six TLDs; shows Verisign uses the notice window fully, so expect the next .com announcement about 6 months before 2027-10-26 or a chosen date near it (April 2027 or so).
8. .ai: expired names now go through the registry's Namecheap auction partnership, not Dynadot (DNW 2026-06-29); the same article notes a continuing wave of first renewals at $160+ per 2 years.
9. Chagos: legislation was shelved after the US withdrew support (Guardian related-story headline dated 2026-04-11, article not opened), payments are on hold, and The Times headline says ministers believe the deal is dead. The "UK will rework" line is accurate but the direction of travel is stall, not progress.
10. Dynadot page currency now renders by geography (CNY for my client), so the dossier's Dynadot USD figures are not reproducible from every location; Porkbun API and Cloudflare mirror are steadier references.
11. Cloudflare's own product page could not be opened (Cloudflare challenge host blocked by egress policy); the Cloudflare price figures still rest on the cfdomainpricing.com mirror. I re-read the mirror today: com 10.46, ai 80, dev 12.2, app 14.2, io 32/50, studio 31.2.
