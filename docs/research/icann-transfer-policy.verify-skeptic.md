# Skeptic verification: ICANN Transfer Policy dossier (task key icann-transfer-policy)

Verifier lens: re-derive from ICANN.org the current Transfer Policy provisions on 60-day locks, TAC validity, and the change-of-registrant (CoR) lock; re-derive the effective dates of amendments; scan 2025 and 2026 ICANN announcements for implementation dates the analyst may have got wrong.
Date of check: 2026-09-29. All fetches were done fresh by me with curl through the agent proxy (browser User-Agent, TLS verification on), plus one headless Chromium render. Raw copies: `working-directory/research/verify_skeptic/`. I did not reuse the analyst's raw files for any verdict. The WebSearch budget for the session was exhausted (200 of 200), so no web searches were possible; everything below is direct fetches. No egress blocks were hit and none were worked around.

Bottom line: the dossier's core findings hold. The Feb-2024 Transfer Policy is the version in force; the 60-day locks and CoR lock are exactly as quoted; the Transfer Policy Review recommendations (TAC, 336 h, 720 h, CoR lock removal) were adopted by the Board on 7 Jun 2026 and have no effective date. I found two corrections (one to the description of the 2024 update, one to a retention-period attribution) and one important primary source the analyst did not cite (ICANN's Consolidated Policy Scorecard of 21 Sep 2026) that independently confirms "no date yet".
