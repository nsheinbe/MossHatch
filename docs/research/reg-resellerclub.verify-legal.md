## 1. Claim-by-claim results

### 1.1 Resale under own brand, price and API checkout is permitted (analyst: "conditional")
Verdict: CONFIRMED for the LogicBoxes template; ResellerClub-specific text UNVERIFIABLE.
Re-derived from:
* Master Agreement (https://demo.myorderbox.com/servlet/ViewAgreementServlet?requestfor=resellermasteragreement&pgaction=back), fetched 200:
  * 3(1) "LogicBoxes grants Contractee a license to use the OrderBox for buying and selling OrderBox Products for the term of this Agreement."
  * 4(1) "The Contractee shall be responsible for providing customer service, billing support, and technical support to their Lower Tier Sub-Resellers & Customers." (verbatim match)
  * 4(5) "...OrderBox Products under this agreement may be made available to Customers, and Lower Tier Sub-Resellers only after they enter into a legally binding agreement which is no less protective of LogicBoxes than this Agreement and which contains the requirements contained herein applicable to the Contractee..." (verbatim match, plus the Contractee "responsible for any liability resulting from Customers' ... noncompliance").
* I searched Master, Domain Extension, Appendix A/B/C and the HTTP API overview for "own use", "internal use", "resell", "white label", "not permitted", "competing": NO clause prohibits resale, white-labelling, or restricts API use to the reseller's own use. Appendix A 1(6) (request volume) and A 2(3) (data use) are the only API-usage limits.
* API overview https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/744: "The HTTP API provides a simple way to integrate the functionality of our Reseller platform with your Storefront, Control Panel or Application." (stronger than what the analyst quoted).
* Startup guide https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq692.html: "Along with your Reseller account, you get a comprehensive HTTP API. All Resellers have API access to the system. Every functionality of the system is available as an API call." and heading "Build your own Control Panels using our HTTP API" (both verbatim).
* NEW: https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq307.html "When you signup for a Reseller Account ... you are automatically signed up for all Products that we offer." So no per-product or per-TLD approval step in the platform. And https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq335.html: the reseller may set its own Sub-Reseller/Customer legal agreements ("Use my own Agreement"), Demo Registrar merely "suggest[s]" keeping the sample text with minor modifications.

### 1.2 Own price; premium names pass-through
CONFIRMED. faq971: "Set your Selling Price for different TLDs that you are selling." (line 1147). https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/1953: "You cannot set the Selling Price for Premium Domains." and "You as a Reseller, your Sub-Reseller(s) and the end Customer will be charged the same Price for a Premium Domain."

### 1.3 Own brand (emails, WHOIS)
CONFIRMED with an added constraint. faq334: "Every e-mail sent to your Sub-Reseller/Customer, however, is sent transparently from YOUR e-mail address. Demo Registrar Name/Address is not mentioned anywhere in this process." Missed constraint: Master 17 (PUBLICITY) says the Contractee "shall not create, publish, distribute, or permit any written / oral / electronic material that makes reference to us or our Service Providers or uses any of LogicBoxes's registered Trademarks ... without first submitting such material to us ... and receiving prior written consent." So naming ResellerClub/LogicBoxes/PDR in marketing or in Mosshatch's own ToS needs written consent (the mandated Registrar Registrant Agreement is the obvious exception). Master 12(2) and RAA 3.12.1 also bar ICANN / "ICANN-Accredited Registrar" logos and claiming to be accredited. Master 24(6): "Contracte[e] will not make any statement, whether on his Website or otherwise, that reasonably would contradict anything in this Agreement."

### 1.4 Registrant of record is the end customer; Mosshatch is not registrant
CONFIRMED (definitional). Extension 1(5): "'Registrant' refers to the registrant/owner of a Domain Order as in the OrderBox Database." Extension 2(2): "The Reseller will not make any changes to any information associated with the Domain Order without explicit authorization from the Registrant of that Domain Order." No clause forbids Mosshatch from being the registrant itself. Note registrant agreement clause "Agents and Licensing": whoever registers on behalf of another "represent[s] and warrant[s] that you have the authority to bind the person or entity listed as registrant", and the listed registrant may move the domain to another account "irrespective of wishes of agent/account owner". Also the end customer contracts DIRECTLY with the registrar (see 1.5), so Mosshatch does not own the registrant relationship exclusively.

### 1.5 Acceptance of the Registrar Registrant Agreement is the reseller's duty
CONFIRMED verbatim. Extension 2(4): "The Reseller acknowledges, agrees and ensures that the Registrant accepts the 'Registrar Registrant Agreement' before purchasing domain names. The Reseller shall display the 'Registrar Registrant Agreement' before collecting payment from the Registrant." faq971 lines 1151-1154: "you need to ensure that you display the Registrar Registrant Agreement ... within the domain name registration buy process on your website" and "It is compulsory to display this document as is to your Customers and get them to agree". https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/835: `v2/commons/legal-agreements.json?type=registrarregistrantagreementv2` returns `productkeys`, `registrar-name`, `agreement` (so there are several registrar-specific agreements keyed by TLD product).
Content of the agreement (https://demo.myorderbox.com/servlet/ViewAgreementServlet?requestfor=registrarregistrantagreementv2&pgaction=back, 200): "Company" = P.D.R Solutions (U.S.) LLC for customers outside India, Endurance International Group (India) Private Limited for India (confirmed). Points the analyst did not report:
* Governing law Commonwealth of Massachusetts (non-India), incorporates PDR's Arbitration Agreement (publicdomainregistry.com/legal, unreadable, 403).
* "Domain Parking": on registration "the domain will be automatically placed on name servers provided by the Company ... 'coming soon' page"; expired names may be pointed to an "expired" page; these pages "may contain advertisements" and "the Company reserves the right to collect and retain all revenue".
* "DNS Wildcard": if you use the Company's DNS management and "fail to configure a wildcard DNS", the Company "may insert wildcard DNS records" pointing unresolved subdomains to a page that "may contain advertisements". This bears directly on the provider-hosted DNS feature.
* "Expired domain names": Company "may, in our sole discretion, renew and transfer the domain name to a third party ... (ED Transfer)"; Assignment clause lets the Company "transfer your domain name from one accredited registrar to another ... without requiring your consent".
* Fee changes on 30 days' notice; 15-day email verification with suspension; 60-day locks (gTLD initial registration/inbound transfer and change of registrant).

### 1.6 Fee display and reminders (Extension 2(5), 2(6))
CONFIRMED verbatim. Also platform side: https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/2309 reminders to registrant 30 and 7 days before expiry; https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/2008 registrant email verification within 15 days else "the domain name would be deactivated" (gTLDs only, "not required for ccTLDs"); https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq423.html annual WHOIS data reminder; faq490/faq524 list compulsory emails.

### 1.7 Term and termination (2 years, auto-renew 1 year, 90 days' notice)
CONFIRMED with added nuance. Master 7: 2-year term, auto-renewal 1-year terms; Contractee may elect not to renew "by giving written notice 30 days in advance"; 8(1)(3): either party may terminate at any time on 90 days' written notice; 8(1)(2): 10-day cure for breach. Missed: 8(4) lets the Contractee terminate a Product Agreement Extension "in the event that the Contractee does not agree with any upward revision to the Pricing ... within 30 days of such revision." Missed: 8(5)(1) LogicBoxes "shall suspend OrderBox Users' access ... upon receiving Termination notice from the Contractee or upon learning of any event, which LogicBoxes reasonably determines, would lead to Termination"; 8(6)(3) LogicBoxes may transfer all Orders of a product to another party if the Contractee discontinues it; 6(8) includes "in case of Termination of this Agreement" among reasons to "take ownership of or transfer any Order". There is no covenant that customer domains stay live or are released to Mosshatch on termination; data export only in CSV/SQL on request within 90 days (8(5)(4)).

### 1.8 Prepaid Advance Account; payment methods
CONFIRMED verbatim. Appendix C 1(1) "Prior to provisioning any OrderBox Products, the Contractee shall maintain an Advance Account with LogicBoxes."; 1(3) insufficient balance means the Order "shall not be processed"; 2(1) "Cheque, Wire Transfer, Demand Draft, and Credit Card only". Also 2(2) exchange rate set by LogicBoxes and "undisputable"; 2(3) card processing charge "at the discretion of LogicBoxes". I found NO minimum-deposit figure anywhere in the Master, the KB pages on adding funds (faq483, faq518) or the startup guide.

### 1.9 Negative balance and chargeback consequences
CONFIRMED on the mechanism, CORRECTED on one phrase.
* Appendix C 1(5): negative balance not remedied "within 10 days" gives LogicBoxes the right to terminate "with immediate effect and without any notice". 1(6): "If a negative balance ... is not cured within 30 days, LogicBoxes may take ownership of all Orders belonging to Contractee in the OrderBox Database." (verbatim). 1(7) set-off against payments from Contractee, Sub-Resellers or Customers.
* Appendix C 2(5): on a chargeback or bounced payment by the Contractee LogicBoxes may suspend access, terminate, "delete, suspend, deny, cancel, modify, or transfer any or all of the Orders", and "Transfer all Orders placed by the Lower Tier Sub-Resellers or Customers of the Contractee to any other party, or under LogicBoxes's account." This is about Mosshatch's OWN funding payment, not end-customer chargebacks.
* Analyst's red flag "debited amounts never refunded": CORRECTED. 4(4) reads "will not refund any amount that has already been debited ... under any circumstances, unless such debit is found to be erroneous." And the KB provides a Money Back Grace Period refund on deletion (https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/servlet/KBServlet/faq1451.html: "you may be entitled to a refund ... However, you may have to bear a deletion charge"). Practical effect: after the grace window Mosshatch absorbs end-customer chargebacks.

### 1.10 90-day unilateral price changes
CONFIRMED, with nuance the analyst did not give. 6(5): "LogicBoxes reserves the right to change pricing, minimum order levels, and discounts, at any time, by providing a 90 day advance notice to the Contractee via email. Such notice period however is NOT applicable to any time-bound special promotional offers of less than 90 days." But 6(7) reserves, "without notice", the right to "modify the content on any page within the OrderBox ... including ... pricing information", and Appendix C 1(2) debits "the then current pricing ... as mentioned in the Contractee Control Panel or on the LogicBoxes Website or during the ordering process". So the 90-day protection is arguably undercut by 6(7)/C 1(2); the only remedy is termination (8(3), 8(4), 15(3)). Agreement revisions themselves (15(2)) are "binding and effective upon notification ... via email or within the Contractee Control Panel".

### 1.11 No-notice freezes, suspension, take ownership
CONFIRMED. 6(8) (very long sole-discretion clause, "without notice or refund ... take ownership of or transfer any Order", ending "or for any other appropriate reason"); Appendix A 1(1), 1(11); 25(1) "immediately, without any notification and without assigning any reasons, suspend / terminate OrderBox Users' access" on mere suspicion of breach (reasons within 5 business days on request). Also missed: 6(6) LogicBoxes "in their sole discretion ... deny any LogicBoxes Order or cancel a LogicBoxes Order within 30 days of processing" (refund less processing charges); 6(3) may "temporarily or permanently cease to make an OrderBox Product available" on "reasonable notification".

### 1.12 Post-expiry monetisation / transfer
CONFIRMED. Master 9(5): LogicBoxes "may intercept any network/communication requests to such LogicBoxes Order ... may choose to monetize such requests ... may choose to delete ... may choose to transfer the ownership of the Order to any third party in their sole discretion". 6(9) redirect to parking pages "as per settings ... made by LogicBoxes or Contractee". KB faq601: expired names are "put on a temporary commercial Parking Page". Registrant agreement ED Transfer (see 1.5).
One correction on wording: dossier says the registry auto-renew "locks reseller funds ('locks our funds')". faq601 says "the Registry auto-renews it for a period of 1 year and locks our funds for the same" where "our" is the provider; it does not say the reseller's Advance Account is locked. Treat as unverified.

### 1.13 Sanctions / OFAC
CONFIRMED and under-stated. Master 4(8), 5(8), 6(12): breach means suspension/termination "without a refund or compensation"; 6(11) ccTLD prohibition for Sanctioned Countries. https://demo.myorderbox.com/kb/answer/2489: "we are unable to support partners and clients from Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria and the Crimea region. These include Resellers, Sub Resellers and Customers." The analyst's quotation stops before "These include Resellers, Sub Resellers and Customers". Consequence: Mosshatch must screen END CUSTOMERS (Prohibited Persons = entities located in sanctioned countries plus OFAC SDNs) at checkout; a violation is a Master breach that can terminate the account with no refund.

### 1.14 "Legal name and country not editable after signup" (program requirement)
CORRECTED. The article the analyst cited, faq1492, is in the "Customer Control Panel Caveats" and reads: "Modification of Legal Name (Company/Name) and/or Country (from within the Customer Control Panel) will not be possible once the sign-up process is complete." The RESELLER-side article is faq1493 ("Restriction on Change of Legal Name"): "At the time of signing up as a Reseller ... care must be taken to mention the correct Legal Name (Company Name). Modification of Legal Name ... (from within the Reseller Control Panel) will not be possible once the sign-up process is complete." So for the reseller account only the legal name is documented as locked (country not stated). Practical: form the Mosshatch legal entity first. Support can change it "in case of a genuine requirement".

### 1.17 ICANN RAA 3.12 flow-down and 3.18 abuse
CONFIRMED text, CORRECTED version. The analyst read the 17 Sep 2013 RAA. ICANN's RAA page (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/accredited-registrars/registrar-accreditation-agreement, 200) now lists the RAA "Updated 21 January 2024", with "The 2024 global amendment to existing registrar accreditation agreements ... effective 5 April 2024". I fetched the current text (https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/registrar-accreditation-agreement-21jan24-en.htm):
* 3.12 and 3.12.1-3.12.7: substantively the same as quoted (3.12.2 now cites "ICANN Registration data lookup tool (https://lookup.icann.org)" instead of InterNIC). Analyst omitted 3.12.3 (resellers must identify the sponsoring registrar on customer inquiry) and 3.12.4 (privacy/proxy specification compliance, relevant if Mosshatch sells privacy).
* 3.18 CHANGED: 3.18.1 abuse contact now covers "DNS Abuse and Illegal Activity", publish "an email address or webform", and "provide the reporter with confirmation that it has received the report"; NEW 3.18.2: on "actionable evidence" of DNS Abuse the registrar "must promptly take the appropriate mitigation action(s)"; the 24x7 law-enforcement contact is now 3.18.3 (was 3.18.2); procedures/records 3.18.4 (was 3.18.3). Because 3.12 requires reseller agreements to enable the registrar to perform all its obligations, expect the live ResellerClub agreement to carry DNS-abuse mitigation language that the 2013-era template lacks.
* ICANN Consensus Policies page (https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/consensus-policies, 200) lists policies the reseller must follow under Extension 2(3): Registration Data Policy (effective 21 Aug 2025; updated 12 May 2026 for Urgent Requests, effective date TBD), Registration Data Marketing Restriction Policy, Registration Data Reminder Policy, Expired Registration Recovery Policy, Domain Name Transfer Policies (marked as updated 21 Feb 2024 for the Registration Data Policy, implementation required by 21 Aug 2025). This page lists no replacement transfer policy, which partly answers the dossier's open item; I did not check the GNSO transfer-policy implementation pipeline.

### 1.18 Reseller Extension 3 (Parent's rights) and indemnities
CONFIRMED verbatim (Extension 3 "freeze, delete, suspend, deny, cancel, modify, take ownership of or transfer any Domain Order"). Indemnity: Extension 2(1) reseller "shall indemnify Parent ... for any liability resulting from Registrants' noncompliance"; Master 11(1) broad indemnity covering breach, the Contractee's products, customer actions, "any action of LogicBoxes as permitted by this Agreement". LogicBoxes' own liability is capped (Master 10) at "total amount paid ... during the most recent three (3) month period" and to the amount paid for the Order in question in litigation. Asymmetric: uncapped indemnity by Mosshatch, capped liability of LogicBoxes. Governing law Singapore, SIAC arbitration seated in Singapore (one arbitrator, Singaporean), Singapore courts, and "LogicBoxes reserves the right to enforce the law in the Country/State/District where the ... Place of Management of the Contractee is situated" (22, 23). Confirmed as template terms; counterparty is "PDR Ltd., a Cayman Islands Limited company".

### 1.24 Data handling
PARTLY COVERED by the analyst (Master 13(1), 13(3)); important gaps. I searched the Master, the Domain Extension and the Customer Master for GDPR, personal data, data protection, processor/controller, SCC, cross-border: NO data-protection, DPA or processor terms exist in the templates (only a reference to a "privacy policy" in the Customer Master's acceptable-use list, and the registrant agreement's link to Endurance's privacy policy). What the templates do say: 4(3) all Customer information "including authentication information is accessible to LogicBoxes and its Service Providers"; 6(2)(3) LogicBoxes may send Contractee info to Service Providers; 6(4); 13(1) LogicBoxes owns "rights, title and interests worldwide in the OrderBox Database, and all data ... derivative works"; 13(2) LogicBoxes may "publish, disclose, use ... for any other appropriate reason"; Appendix A 2(3) reseller may use OrderBox data only "To perform services contemplated" and "To communicate with the Lower Tier Sub-Reseller or Customer of an Order with respect to the Order, Support thereof, or any other matter pertaining to Parent or its services", never "Mass Mailing or SPAM" or "Selling the data" (so Mosshatch's own marketing use of this data is not clearly allowed). Any EU/UK customer base needs a separately negotiated DPA.
