## Headline

The analyst's core legal finding stands: the RSA does grant a right to resell worldwide under the reseller's own prices, brand and checkout, and the API Access Agreement defers resale use of the API to the RSA. Confirmed from the primary text. Two things are overstated or under-covered:

1. The TL;DR says "the contract is clean". It is permissive on the right to resell but heavily one-sided (see section C). "Clean" should be "permits resale, but reseller-hostile".
2. The dossier missed several clauses that matter to a launch decision: an attached Data Processing Addendum (24-hour breach notice, Annex 3 security controls, delete-or-return on termination that collides with the 7-year retention duty), a one-sided consequential-damages waiver in the current RSA, an uncapped reseller indemnity, no price-change notice or price lock anywhere in the RSA, Name.com's own no-cause 30-day termination and 30-day discretionary discontinuation rights over each end customer's registration, the family of six sponsoring registrars, the RAA 3.7.7.3 licensee-liability rule behind the "reseller as registrant" risk, and the absence of .ai from Name.com's Additional Registry Requirements page.

## A. Resale right and program (claims re-derived)

A1. RSA grants resale right. CONFIRMED. DG18NICY.js, section "RESELLING THE SERVICES": "Name.com grants You a non-exclusive, fully-paid-up, royalty-free, terminable, non-transferable right and license to resell the Services worldwide." Also: "The Services may only be accessed through the application programming interface ... Your Account, websites created by Name.com which use the API" (section 5 Technology), so API ordering is the sanctioned channel. No exclusivity, minimum volume, non-compete, or white-label prohibition appears anywhere in the current RSA (I searched the whole text for exclusiv/compet/minimum/volume/quota/commit/white-label/brand). Acceptance is by conduct: "By reselling the Services or offering the Services for resale, You acknowledge that You have read ... and agree", "effective upon Your acceptance ... as electronically recorded by Name.com". First para also says: "Unless You and Name.com have executed another agreement governing Your resale" - a negotiated agreement supersedes the RSA (useful lever).

A2. Own prices; default cost is standard retail. CONFIRMED verbatim (RSA Payment Terms): "You may set Your own prices ... Unless otherwise communicated to You in writing by Name.com, You will be charged Name.com's standard retail price". Verified by absence: the RSA contains no price-change notice period, no price lock and no rate-card mechanism (grep for price/pricing/discount/wholesale hits only this clause). Renewal prices float with Name.com's retail list. The Registration Agreement's "30 days prior notice of fee changes on the Pricing Page" is "reasonable efforts" and is owed to registrants, not the RSA.
  Also required and not in the analyst summary: the reseller "must publish on Your website all fees for domain name registrations, renewals, post-expiration renewal fees (if different), and also redemption/restore fees" and repeat them in Your Additional Terms.

A3. Customer acceptance and record duties. CONFIRMED: "You and each of Your end customers ... must explicitly agree to the Domain Name Registration Agreement ... and You must retain a written record of such explicit acceptance". Privacy: customers must also agree to the Whois Privacy Service Agreement (DPSI, last updated 25 April 2018). Audit clause: term + 7 years for registration correspondence and account/payment records; produce "within two (2) business days"; failure is a material breach. Indemnity for failing to obtain consent.

A4. Additional Terms. CORRECTED (minor). Analyst summary says they "must 'identify Name.com, Inc. as the sponsoring registrar'". Actual text: "must also identify Name.com, Inc. as the sponsoring registrar or provide a means to Your Customers to identify the sponsoring registrar", and "you must provide the identity of the sponsoring registrar upon an inquiry". This matters because the Registration Agreement footer lists six accredited registrars (Name.com, Inc. 625; Name.net, Inc. 826; DomainSite, Inc. 466; Name105, Inc. 452; Name106, Inc. 1040; Name117, Inc. 1547) and says "any one of which will be the registrar for your domain name". I checked IANA registrar-ids.xml (updated 2026-09-23): all six are status Accredited, with RDAP at namerdap.systems. So "Name.com, Inc., IANA 625" is not guaranteed to be the sponsoring registrar for every name; offering a lookup link satisfies the RSA and mirrors RAA 3.12.2.

A5. Sub-resellers. CONFIRMED with flow-down: a written Sub-Reseller Agreement must bind them to the RSA, require their customers to accept the Reseller Agreement, Registration Agreement and privacy agreement, and give Name.com and the registry operator a survival-of-termination indemnity; the reseller is liable for their acts.

A6. API Access Agreement tracks. CONFIRMED and EXTENDED. DffIiKU_.js: Reseller Terms defer to the RSA. Non-Reseller Terms: "You are not permitted to access or use the API for the purpose of reselling ... You represent and warrant that You will only use the API for the purpose of managing Your domain names" (the "own use only" clause; the analyst quoted only the first half). Other terms the analyst did not surface: Access Form liability cap = amounts received "over the term of this Access Form"; broad indemnity "in connection with, arising out of, or relating to Your access to and use of the API" with no fault or carve-out; governing law Washington with "exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of King County" and fee-shifting for Name.com (in conflict with the RSA's JAMS arbitration); "terminate ... or suspend ... access ... at any time and for any reason" (CONFIRMED); modifications effective 30 days after notice (CONFIRMED). The Access Form's cross-reference to "Section 4 and Section 7 of the RSA" for acceptable use points at the legacy RSA numbering (Technology = 4, Restrictions = 7); the same page carries a legacy "Reseller Agreement for resellers onboarded prior to 5 December 2022". In the current RSA those are Sections 5 and 9. Unverified: which track a self-serve token click-through binds a new account to (needs a logged-in view). Action: get written confirmation from reseller@name.com that the account is on the Reseller track under the RSA before launch.

A7. "No fees, no application, no sales call" vs "select number ... signed Access Form". CONFIRMED as a real conflict. Marketing (https://www.name.com/nameapi, fetched today): "No fees, no application, no sales call." and "Custom rate cards available for high-volume partnerships." Docs "Getting Started" (https://docs.name.com/guides/getting-started.md): create account, then Account Settings -> Security -> API Tokens, issue dev and production tokens; no approval step mentioned. Contract: "permits a select number of Name.com customers ... You must return a completed and signed copy of this Access Form". Approval-gated endpoints confirmed in the OpenAPI ("only available to approved reseller accounts. Contact name.com support to request access": create sub-account, verify contact, premium lists; internal transfer-in "Restricted to approved enterprise resellers"). Practical onboarding = self-serve, but Name.com retains discretion. Also confirmed the Quickstart says "some reseller accounts are approved to verify contacts via API; most are not".

A8. Payment prerequisites. CONFIRMED. RSA: "required to maintain a positive and sufficient account balance or set up a valid payment profile, such as a credit card"; "may require ... a particular payment means, such as by wire transfer"; "may also demand reasonable assurance of payment, at Name.com's sole discretion". Funding guide: "To increase the default purchase limit above $2,500, contact reseller@name.com" (whether the limit is per order or cumulative is not stated - unverifiable); ACH "up to $20,000 per transaction"; ACH "must be enabled ... Contact reseller@name.com" (analyst omitted this gate). No minimum deposit or volume commitment appears in the RSA, Access Form or docs.

A9. Reseller Pricing Program. CONFIRMED with corrections. BlG9FYeC.js (Last Updated: August 2024): Bronze $1,000 and 100 domain registrations; Silver $2,500 and 250; Gold $10,000 and 1,000. Corrections/nuance: (a) "Qualifying Total" = last-365-day spend on Qualifying Products PLUS current Account credit at enrolment, so a prefunded balance counts toward the spend leg (the registrations leg must still be 100 new registrations; renewals and transfers excluded); (b) eligibility is per Account, no aggregation across accounts; (c) discounts run one year from enrolment with an annual review and automatic tier drop; (d) "we may modify or cancel the Program or any of the features ... without notice, unless otherwise explicitly stated" but threshold changes get "at least 30 days' notice", other terms change "at Name.com's sole discretion and without notice"; "Name.com is the sole arbiter of eligibility" (CONFIRMED); (e) "you may receive the lower of promotional pricing or Program Discounts" (no stacking); (f) "You may join the Program online at name.com/resellersolutions" (that URL serves the same page as /nameapi). Discount percentages are not published anywhere I could reach (docs, policy page, /nameapi, /pricing): stays unverified.

A10. KYC. UNVERIFIABLE (absence). No reseller KYC form found. Sanctions clause CONFIRMED (RSA section 10: reject/terminate "and freeze Your account and all associated funds, without notice" if Name.com "cannot confirm" a person is not a Sanctioned Person). Registration Agreement: "at any time and at our sole discretion, request ... passport or identification documentation". Additional reseller warranties: entity "duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing".

## B. Registrant of record, notices, abuse, legal process

B1. Registrant of record. CONFIRMED. Registration Agreement: "you" = "the registrant listed in the WHOIS/Registration Data Directory Service ('RDDS') contact information". OpenAPI: omitted contacts default to the account's contacts. Quickstart: "set the reseller (you) as the domain contacts to move fast". Registrant Communications guide (fetched): "the reseller is the legal owner of the domain in ICANN and registry systems ... legal notices (e.g., cease-and-desist letters, takedown requests, and potential lawsuits) are served to the reseller". "Organization field ... is considered the legal Registered Name Holder".
  MISSED legal basis: Registration Agreement section 13 "Agents and Licenses" - "If you license the use of a domain name you register ... to a third-party, you remain the domain name holder of record ... you are liable for harm caused by wrongful use of the domain name, unless you disclose within 7 days the licensor's identity and current contact information" - this implements RAA 3.7.7.3 (I re-read the 2013 RAA text on icann.org). Also: "If you are registering a domain name for or on behalf of someone else, you represent that you have the authority to and will bind that person as a principal". And only the registrant and admin contact listed in RDDS can approve a transfer. Also a flag for counsel: the ICANN Specification on Privacy and Proxy Registrations (RAA, still in force until the accreditation program exists) defines a "Proxy Service" as an RNH licensing use to a customer while the RNH's contact is shown in RDDS; a Mosshatch-as-registrant model resembles that, and the RSA (section 6.C) makes the reseller comply with the Specification. Not a conclusion, a question for counsel.

B2. ICANN notice routes. CONFIRMED (Registrant Communications and Contact Verification guides): Name.com sends by default; white-labelling "available upon request"; or "You send your own emails (requires an addendum assuming email responsibility, plus logging evidence)". Logs: registration life "and for at least two (2) years thereafter"; sample logs and sample emails before enablement; described as an "approved / contracted" workflow.

B3. Abuse and legal process. CONFIRMED and one "unverified" item resolved. Support and Abuse Policies page (chunk DzyvWnVt.js): abuse form, abuse@name.com, +1 720.249.2374, handled by Compliance and Abuse "in conjunction with our legal team", "may involve facilitating communication with the registered domain holder". Verisign RDAP (rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/name.com) shows registrar Name.com, Inc. with abuse contact abuse@name.com / tel 7202492374. RSA support clause: Name.com forwards third-party communications "at Name.com's discretion" and "reserves the right to respond to such communications directly". Subpoena Policy CONFIRMED (Corporation Service Company, 112 N Curry St., Carson City NV; may notify the customer; may charge the customer's account an administration fee). Name.com support targets (first reply 12h tickets, 24h resolution) are "goals", not contract terms.

## C. Risk clauses (claims re-derived)

C1. 30-day no-cause termination by either party. CONFIRMED (RSA section 10). Notice to Name.com must go to the website address or notices@name.com. Also confirmed: immediate termination "without notice or right to cure" for violations of section 6 (ICANN obligations) and section 9 (restrictions on use); and a broader right to "terminate this RSA immediately if Name.com determines, in its sole discretion, that You, Your Sub-Resellers or Your Customers have failed to comply with any term or condition of this RSA or the Name.com Registration Agreement". Extra grounds: failure to respond to Name.com within five (5) calendar days; inadequate support ("excessive number of support calls directly from Your Customers"); immediate suspension of API/resale "immediately and without notice" if use "risks ... degradation"; "Repetitive, high volume inquires or other excessive use" is on the prohibited list (section 9) and carries no-cure termination; drop-catching prohibited. No refund on for-cause suspension.

C2. Amendments. CORRECTED. Summary says "effective 30 days after posting". Text: non-material changes take effect on posting; material changes take effect at the earlier of 30 days after posting or "upon Your acceptance ... including by continuing Your performance under this RSA after you have been notified"; ICANN/registry-driven changes "may become effective fewer than thirty (30) days". Sole remedy is to terminate. The RSA page carries no version or effective date (the DPA says December 5, 2022), so changes cannot be dated from the page.

C3. Chargebacks. CONFIRMED and extended: chargeback by You or any customer below the account is a material breach, "jointly and severally" liable, $35.00 per incident plus merchant fees, Name.com may suspend all accounts and "assume all right, title, interest in, and use of any domain name registration(s) ... (the 'Collateral')", sell/retain it "even if You assert that the value of the Collateral exceeds the amount You owe", reinstatement fee "currently set at US$200".

C4. Liability. CORRECTED framing. The 12-month cap is Name.com's cap only ("Name.com's aggregate liability ... shall not exceed the aggregate amount received ... over the twelve (12) month period", and per-transaction fees for a single component). The reseller's indemnity (breach; any product or service of Yours; use of the Services; domain business) is uncapped and has no negligence carve-out. In the current RSA the consequential-damages waiver protects Name.com only ("NAME.COM SHALL NOT BE LIABLE ..."); the legacy RSA text on the same page says "NEITHER PARTY". JAMS arbitration, King County, Washington, Washington law: CONFIRMED for the RSA.

C5. Data protection. NOT IN ANALYST SUMMARY. The RSA includes a Data Processing Addendum (LAST UPDATED: December 5, 2022; reseller = processor, Name.com = controller; "in conflict ... this DPA will take precedence"): breach notice "no later than 24 hours after detection" to legal@name.com cc privacy@name.com; Annex 3 minimum security measures (SSAE 16/ISAE 2403 / ISO / NIST-equivalent controls, MFA, annual external pen tests, FIPS 140-2 aligned crypto, background checks, customer breach notice within 48 hours); Registrar audit rights (SOC/ISO reports accepted in lieu); on termination "irrevocably delete (and provide adequate certification of such deletion) or return, in full, all Shared Personal Data to the Registrar". That last duty collides with the RSA Audit clause (retain records 7 years after term); the DPA prevails on conflict. The DPA still cites Privacy Shield (dated). SCC PDF exists (8 pages, HTTP 200, /files/NameResellerDPASCCs(August18,2022).pdf); its text was not read (no PDF renderer). The Registration Agreement also has an "Ownership of information and data" clause: Name.com owns the domain database and the registrant/contact data (footnote 1 says this denotes controller responsibility), so Mosshatch must keep its own copy of customer records.

C6. Fee, refund and lifecycle terms. CONFIRMED. Refund Policy (LAST UPDATED: April 1, 2026): 5-day window at Name.com's "sole discretion"; renewals never refundable; premium never refundable; redemption fee non-refundable; .AI and .IO on the never-refundable list. Registration Agreement: all fees non-refundable unless stated; ~25-day "reactivation period" (may be omitted), may park, may change RDDS registrant of the expired name, may auction expired names with no proceeds to the holder, may skip RGP for ccTLDs. Docs (domain-renewals-expiration.md): Guaranteed Renewal Window ~25 days, then off the API, extended renewal (~days 26-43) and redemption "UI only ... not guaranteed" - CONFIRMED.
  MISSED: (a) Registration Agreement lets Name.com "terminate the Services thirty (30) days after we send notice ... at any time and for any reason" (pro-rata refund) and "reject ... or elect to discontinue providing Services ... for any reason within thirty (30) days of a Service initiation or a Service renewal" - a per-domain no-cause exit that sits on top of the RSA; (b) AUP: Name.com "may suspend, cancel, transfer or modify" services or domains "without prior notice"; (c) Registration Agreement end-customer liability cap of US$400; (d) the Registration Agreement is drafted for direct account holders ("You must create an account"), and Your Additional Terms lose any conflict, so Mosshatch refund or cancellation promises that exceed the Registration Agreement are subordinate as a matter of contract (consumer-law point for counsel).

C7. Other clauses worth a launch checklist (missed): trademarks usable only "as pre-approved in writing" (no "powered by name.com" logo without consent); "shall not branch or otherwise prepare derivatives of the API" (ambiguous for generated clients; the official SDKs are MIT); Confidentiality (term + 3 years) covers "costs, prices", so a negotiated rate card would be Name.com's confidential information and may not be publishable in a "wholesale plus flat fee" pitch; taxes: fees exclude taxes and API responses show `TotalPaid` "including VAT when applicable"; Name.com may "directly engage with and provide services to any of Your Customers" if the reseller is terminated, unresponsive or stops using the account; assignment of the RSA needs Name.com's consent (matters for an acquisition of Mosshatch); RSA asks reseller to publish/link ICANN Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities and the ICANN educational page and not to display the ICANN logo.

## D. ICANN flow-down (claims re-derived)

D1. RSA section 6 mirrors 2013 RAA 3.12.1-3.12.7 (no ICANN logo; registration agreement provisions and notices required by RAA/consensus policies; identify sponsoring registrar or provide a means; proxy/privacy specification; ICANN education link; Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities). Verified against https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/approved-with-specs-2013-09-17-en (the page is labelled an archive of the 2013 RAA; the RSA links to it; the current RAA is at /resources/pages/registrars/registrars-en and was not diffed). RAA 3.12 also says the registrar "must enter into written agreements with all of its Resellers" and is responsible for reseller performance, which explains the immediate-termination clauses.
D2. Registration Data Policy. CONFIRMED: effective 21 August 2025, revised 12 May 2026 (icann.org page). It has an optional "Reseller" data element (registrar MAY generate/publish); no direct reseller obligations found beyond what flows through the registrar.
D3. Registry flow-down. MISSED GAP: RSA "Additional Registry Requirements" says to include registry-required terms "found in" https://www.name.com/policies/additional-registry-requirements. I fetched that page (66 KB of text). Entries for launch TLDs: .app and .dev -> registry.google policy links; .studio -> identity.digital policies; .io -> https://www.nic.io/terms.htm; .com -> "N/A". **.ai has no entry at all** (the alphabetical list goes .ag, .agency, .airforce, .am) and .ai is not labelled a "Registration Partner" TLD. Name.com sells .ai (price feed) but its own flow-down page gives no registry terms for it; Mosshatch will have to obtain the .ai registry terms directly.
