Storage and cookies

Cookies

Mosshatch sets one session cookie when you sign in, so the site knows it is you. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not shared. During sign-in a short-lived cookie holds the sign-in step you are on.

hatchkind.com sets no cookies at all.

Browser storage

Your browser stores three preferences: calm mode, sound, and how long a revealed value stays visible. Nothing else is stored, and never a secret.

Page counts

We count page views with Vercel Web Analytics. The script is served from this site. It sets no cookie. The address it sends has no query string and no token. We do not load advertising scripts.
