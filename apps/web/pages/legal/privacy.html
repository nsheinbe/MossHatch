How the preview works today

  • To show whether a name is already registered, our server asks the registry's public RDAP service. The registry sees the name, not your address. We don't write your searches to our database or logs; see our commitments.
  • Registrant contact details are encrypted field by field, under a key apart from the vault.
  • A public card shows the domain name, its creature's traits and the hatch date. It never shows your name, email or any text you typed.

First-party conversion measurement

We count visits, searches, available results, selections and checkout starts in aggregate by day, preview or live mode, broad device size and limited campaign labels. We do not include search text, full URLs, raw IP addresses, cookies or a persistent visitor identifier in these counts. A page load is not a unique person.

When you start checkout, we may associate those campaign labels with your order to measure confirmed registrations and payments. Your order remains private. The browser never determines whether a purchase succeeded. We honor browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals for this optional measurement.
