How the preview works today

  • To show whether a name is already registered, our server asks the registry's public RDAP service. The registry sees the name, not your address. We don't write your searches to our database or logs; see our commitments.
  • Registrant contact details are encrypted field by field, under a key apart from the vault.
  • A public card shows the domain name, its creature's traits and the hatch date. It never shows your name, email or any text you typed.
