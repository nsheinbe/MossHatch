Legal-process request policy

Counsel is drafting this document. Until it is published, this page only lists what it will cover. Nothing here is a promise, a term of sale or legal advice.

What it will cover

  • What we can decrypt: the Nest vault is encrypted on our servers, not end-to-end, so a court order could reach its contents.
  • Who decides whether a request is valid.
  • When we tell you about a request, unless the law forbids it.
  • How each disclosure is recorded.
