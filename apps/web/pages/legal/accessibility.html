Accessibility statement

Last reviewed 30 September 2026.

Our target

We build to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We fix problems in the code, and we do not use an overlay widget.

How we check

  • Automated checks with axe on every page and view, with the WCAG 2.2 rules turned on.
  • A keyboard pass and a screen-reader pass each phase.

What we know is missing

  • Text drawn over the animated grove is checked by hand, because automated tools cannot judge it.
  • Hold-to-reveal is an enhancement; every reveal also has a single-activation path.
  • The screen-reader pass has covered Chromium only. Safari with VoiceOver, JAWS and NVDA are not yet tested.

Tell us

Write to support@mosshatch.com with the page and what went wrong. We answer within two business days.
