Our commitments

Draft. Counsel has not reviewed this wording.

Your searches

  • We never register a name because you searched for it, and we never hold a name you looked at.
  • We never sell or share what you search for.
  • We do not write the names you search to our database, logs or audit records. An answer is kept in memory only, so a repeat search does not ask again: for at most a minute when our registrar answered, and for at most ten minutes in the preview. Rate limits count searches per address, not names.
  • This website sends a name to our server inside the request, not in its web address, so the name does not appear in the request logs our host (Vercel) keeps. Our command-line tool and agent connections still ask with the name in the address, so those requests can appear in our host's request logs for as long as it keeps them.
  • If you shortlist names or leave a checkout unfinished, this browser tab remembers those names, and the unfinished order's reference, until you close the tab, so you can come back to them. They are not sent to us.
  • Automated tests that run on every change check that a searched name reaches no database table, no log line of ours and no audit row.

Who sees a search

In the preview there is no registrar, and the prices shown are published test prices. To say whether a name is already registered, our server asks each extension's registry through its public RDAP service: Verisign for .com, Google Registry for .dev and .app, and Identity Digital for .ai, .io and .studio. Those registries see the name you typed and our server's address, not yours. Where sales are switched on, checking whether a name is free sends it to our registrar partner (Openprovider), which asks the registry. They see the name. Your internet provider's resolver can see lookups too. We cannot promise what they do with it; we can promise what we do.

How we check availability

We check a name only after you stop typing. Where sales are switched on, we check only through our registrar's own availability check; each visitor and each account has a limit per hour, and the whole service has a daily ceiling. In the preview, a search asks each registry's public RDAP service at most once per extension, and only whether the name is registered: an answer of "registered" shows the name as taken, "not found" shows it as looking unregistered, and anything else as "couldn't check". Each network has a limit per minute and per hour, and each of our servers has a daily ceiling. We do not send your searches to public WHOIS or RDAP services in bulk; their terms forbid high-volume automated use, and we respect that.
