Report abuse

You do not need an account to report a problem.

A domain registered through Mosshatch

For phishing, malware, botnets, pharming or spam, email abuse@mosshatch.com with the domain name, what you saw, when, and any evidence such as URLs or message headers.

A card on hatchkind.com

Email abuse@mosshatch.com with the card's address (for example hatchkind.com/example.com) and why it should come down. For a copyright claim, write to dmca@mosshatch.com. For a trade-mark claim or a request from an authority, write to legal@mosshatch.com.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your report within two business days.
  • We decide within three business days after that, and tell you what we did.
  • When a card report is valid, we take the card down first and look into it second.

Read the abuse, take-down and DMCA procedure for the details.
