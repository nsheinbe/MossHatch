Waitlist privacy note

This note covers only the waitlist for the Mosshatch preview. The full privacy notice is being drafted.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • If you give them: the name you hatched in the preview, and your yes, no or maybe to one question about a paid creature.
  • The exact consent sentence you ticked (as a fingerprint), and when you joined, confirmed, left or were invited.
  • A one-way hash of your network (the first three parts of an IPv4 address), used to limit sign-ups. Never your IP address.

Why

  • To confirm the address is yours, tell you your place in line, and send your invite when it is your turn.
  • To send occasional product news, because you ticked the box that asks for it.
  • To learn, in counts only, which names and extensions people want and whether a paid creature interests them.

How long, and how to leave

  • Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 30 days.
  • Every email has a one-click link to leave the list. Leaving stops all waitlist email and cancels any invite not yet used.
  • To have your entry deleted, write to support@mosshatch.com.
  • Emails are sent by our email provider (Resend) from send.mosshatch.com. We never sell or share the list.

Joining the waitlist does not reserve or register a domain name.

Mosshatch is a domain reseller, not an ICANN-accredited registrar. See registrar of record and your rights.
