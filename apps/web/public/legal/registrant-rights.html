Registrants' rights and registrar of record

Who registers your name

Mosshatch is a reseller. It is not accredited by ICANN. The registrar of record for names you buy here is our registrar partner. You can see the registrar of any name with ICANN's registration data lookup.

The registrar of record is Hosting Concepts B.V., trading as Registrar.eu and Openprovider (IANA ID 1647), of Rotterdam, the Netherlands, through its Openprovider reseller service.

Your rights and responsibilities

ICANN publishes the Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities and educational material for registrants. They apply to every name you register here.

Complaints

Write to support@mosshatch.com. If we cannot resolve it, write to legal@mosshatch.com. You can also complain to ICANN through its compliance pages.

Mosshatch is a domain reseller, not an ICANN-accredited registrar. See registrar of record and your rights.
