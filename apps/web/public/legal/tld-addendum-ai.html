Two years at a time

.ai is registered and renewed for at least 2 years. There is no 1-year option. The price at checkout is the total for 2 years.

Your contact details are public

.ai has no contact privacy. Your name, organisation and contact details show in public lookups.

No refunds

A .ai registration cannot be refunded once it is registered.

The registry's rules

.ai is a country-code extension run for the Government of Anguilla. It is outside ICANN's policies for generic extensions. Abusive use is prohibited, and disputes follow the UDRP. The registry has not published what happens to an expired .ai name, so renew it before the expiry date.
