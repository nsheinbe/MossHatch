Abuse, take-down and DMCA procedure

Counsel is drafting this document. Until it is published, this page only lists what it will cover. Nothing here is a promise, a term of sale or legal advice.

What it will cover

  • How to send a report (see report abuse) and what to include.
  • Our designated agent for copyright notices under the DMCA, once registered with the U.S. Copyright Office.
  • The repeat-infringer policy, counter-notices and put-back.
  • A notice-and-action route for reports from the European Union, if counsel finds it applies.

How the preview works today

  • Reports go to abuse@mosshatch.com, copyright notices to dmca@mosshatch.com. No account is needed.
  • Our own targets: we acknowledge a report within two business days and decide within three.
  • The first action on a valid notice about a card is to take the card down. A person whose cards are taken down twice in a year can no longer publish cards.
  • The DMCA agent is not registered yet.
